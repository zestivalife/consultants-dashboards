# Purpose

The Enterprise Registry Governance Standard establishes the governance framework for all enterprise registries within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

It defines governance principles, ownership models, policies, controls, approval mechanisms, compliance requirements and operational oversight necessary to ensure that every registry remains accurate, trustworthy, secure and continuously aligned with enterprise architecture.

Registry Governance SHALL act as the enterprise authority responsible for maintaining the integrity of all registry assets.

---

# Objectives

The Registry Governance Framework SHALL:

- Govern all enterprise registries.
- Establish ownership accountability.
- Standardise governance workflows.
- Ensure registry integrity.
- Maintain enterprise compliance.
- Protect registry security.
- Enable policy enforcement.
- Support autonomous governance.
- Improve operational transparency.
- Enable continuous governance improvement.

# Governance Principles

## Principle 1 — Governance by Default

Every registry SHALL operate under approved governance policies.

---

## Principle 2 — Accountability

Every registry asset SHALL have clearly identified accountable owners.

---

## Principle 3 — Trustworthiness

Registry information SHALL remain authoritative and verifiable.

---

## Principle 4 — Policy Enforcement

Enterprise policies SHALL be automatically enforced whenever possible.

---

## Principle 5 — Continuous Governance

Registry governance SHALL be continuously monitored and improved.
