# AI_OBSERVABILITY_STANDARD

## Purpose

Establish the enterprise standard for observing, monitoring and analysing the operational health of technology platforms, AI services and business capabilities through unified telemetry, metrics, logs, traces and operational intelligence.

Observability enables proactive detection, diagnosis and optimisation of enterprise operations.

---

## Objectives

- Establish enterprise-wide observability standards.
- Provide complete operational visibility.
- Detect operational anomalies proactively.
- Enable faster incident diagnosis.
- Improve service reliability.
- Measure business health continuously.
- Monitor AI operational behaviour.
- Support evidence-based operational decisions.
- Standardise enterprise dashboards.
- Enable operational analytics and reporting.

---

## Scope

This standard applies to:

- Infrastructure

- Applications
- APIs
- Microservices
- Databases
- Networks
- Containers

- Business Transactions

## Telemetry Standards

Enterprise telemetry shall include:

### Metrics

Quantitative operational measurements.

Examples:

- CPU
- Memory
- Latency
- Error Rate
- Throughput
- Availability

---

### Logs

Structured operational events.

Logs shall include:

- Timestamp
- Severity
- Service
- Environment
- Correlation ID
- User Context (where applicable)
- Error Details

---

### Traces

Distributed transaction tracing shall capture:

- Request Flow
- Service Dependencies
- Response Time
- Processing Time
- External Calls

---

### Events

Business and system events shall be captured for operational analysis.

Examples:

- User Registered
- Payment Completed
- Order Created
- AI Recommendation Generated
- Notification Sent
