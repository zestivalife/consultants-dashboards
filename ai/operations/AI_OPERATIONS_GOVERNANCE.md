# AI_OPERATIONS_GOVERNANCE.md

## Purpose

Establish the enterprise operational governance model for the organisation by defining how production systems, business services, AI capabilities, operational teams, and supporting technologies are operated, governed, monitored, reviewed and continuously improved throughout their operational lifecycle.

This document establishes a single operational framework that enables the organisation to run technology as a business capability rather than as isolated infrastructure or applications.

---

## Objectives

The Operations Governance Standard shall:

- Establish a unified enterprise operations model.
- Define operational governance across all environments.
- Standardise operational ownership and accountability.
- Ensure stable, secure and reliable business operations.
- Govern day-to-day production operations.
- Standardise operational decision making.
- Govern operational risks.
- Establish enterprise operational reviews.
- Define operational performance measurements.
- Govern operational reporting.
- Ensure continuous operational improvement.
- Integrate Engineering, Security, Delivery and Business Operations into one operational ecosystem.

---

# Enterprise Operational Lifecycle

Every operational activity shall follow a common lifecycle.

1. Monitor
2. Observe
3. Detect
4. Analyse
5. Decide
6. Execute
7. Verify
8. Recover (when required)
9. Review
10. Improve

This lifecycle applies consistently across all operational capabilities.
