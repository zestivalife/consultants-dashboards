# AI_OPERATIONAL_INTELLIGENCE_STANDARD

## Purpose

Establish the enterprise standard for transforming operational data into actionable intelligence by providing unified visibility into business operations, platform health, AI performance and enterprise decision-making.

Operational Intelligence enables leadership and operational teams to make informed, evidence-based decisions through real-time analytics, dashboards and business insights.

---

## Objectives

- Establish enterprise operational intelligence.
- Standardise operational analytics.
- Enable real-time business visibility.
- Measure operational performance.
- Monitor business outcomes.
- Analyse AI operational behaviour.
- Support executive decision-making.
- Standardise enterprise dashboards.
- Enable predictive operational insights.
- Drive continuous operational improvement.

---

## Scope

This standard applies to:

- Enterprise Operations
- Business Operations
- AI Operations
- Customer Operations
- Platform Operations
- Engineering Operations
- Executive Reporting
- Business Analytics
- Enterprise Dashboards
- Operational Reporting

---

## Operational Intelligence Principles

### Business-Driven Intelligence

Operational intelligence shall measure business success rather than technical activity alone.

---

### Evidence-Based Decisions

Operational decisions shall be supported by validated data and measurable evidence.

---

### Unified Visibility

Operational intelligence shall provide a single view across business, technology and AI.

---

### Real-Time Awareness

Critical operational information shall be available in near real time.

---

### Actionable Insights

Operational intelligence shall focus on enabling informed action rather than simply presenting data.

---

### Continuous Measurement

Operational performance shall be measured continuously.
