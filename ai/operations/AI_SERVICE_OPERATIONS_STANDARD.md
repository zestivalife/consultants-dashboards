# AI_SERVICE_OPERATIONS_STANDARD

## Purpose

Establish the enterprise standard for managing the day-to-day operation of business and technology services to ensure they remain secure, available, reliable and aligned with business objectives throughout their operational lifecycle.

Service Operations provides the operational discipline required to deliver consistent, high-quality services while enabling continuous improvement and customer satisfaction.

---

## Objectives

- Standardise enterprise service operations.
- Ensure stable day-to-day service delivery.
- Maintain service availability.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Deliver consistent customer experience.
- Standardise operational procedures.
- Coordinate operational activities.
- Support business continuity.
- Enable operational transparency.
- Drive continual service improvement.

---

## Scope

This standard applies to:

- Business Services
- Enterprise Applications
- AI Services
- APIs
- Microservices
- Cloud Platforms
- Infrastructure Services
- Customer Support Services
- Internal Shared Services
- Third-party Managed Services

## Operational Activities

Daily operational activities shall include:

- Service Health Verification
- Operational Monitoring
- Incident Review
- Capacity Review
- Scheduled Maintenance
- Operational Reporting
- Customer Support
- AI Service Verification

---

## Service Request Management

Service requests shall follow a defined lifecycle:

1. Request Submission
2. Classification
3. Prioritisation
4. Assignment
5. Fulfilment
6. Validation
7. Closure

Service requests shall be tracked and measured for performance.

---

## Operational Procedures

Operational procedures shall be:

- Documented
- Version Controlled
- Standardised
- Reviewed Periodically
- Easily Accessible

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) shall exist for all critical operational activities.

---

## Operational Scheduling

Scheduled operational activities shall include:

- Maintenance Windows
- Health Checks
- Backup Verification
- Capacity Reviews
- Security Validation
- Operational Reporting

Schedules shall minimise business disruption.

---

## Customer Support

Customer support shall provide:

- Issue Resolution
- Service Guidance
- Escalation Management
- Status Communication
- Knowledge Assistance

Support performance shall be continuously monitored.

## Vendor & Third-Party Operations
