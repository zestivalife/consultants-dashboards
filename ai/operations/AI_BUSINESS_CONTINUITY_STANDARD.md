# AI_BUSINESS_CONTINUITY_STANDARD

## Purpose

Establish the enterprise standard for ensuring that critical business operations continue during and after disruptive events through effective preparedness, resilience, recovery planning and continuity management.

Business Continuity ensures that essential business capabilities remain operational with minimal disruption regardless of technology failures, cyber incidents, natural disasters, human errors or other unforeseen events.

---

## Objectives

- Establish enterprise business continuity practices.
- Protect critical business operations.
- Minimise operational disruption.
- Ensure continuity of essential services.
- Standardise continuity planning.
- Improve organisational resilience.
- Define recovery priorities.
- Coordinate crisis response.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable continuous organisational preparedness.

---

## Scope

This standard applies to:

- Business Operations
- Enterprise Platforms
- AI Services
- Customer Services
- Cloud Infrastructure
- Data Services
- Corporate Functions
- Third-party Services
- Critical Business Processes
- Operational Support Functions

---

## Business Continuity Principles

### Business First

Business continuity shall prioritise the protection of critical business capabilities and customer commitments.

---

### Preparedness

Continuity planning shall be completed before disruption occurs.

---

### Resilience

Enterprise operations shall withstand disruptions with minimal business impact.

---

### Risk-Based Planning

Continuity strategies shall be based on assessed business and operational risks.

---

### Coordinated Response

Business and technology teams shall operate through a unified continuity framework.

---

### Continuous Readiness

Business continuity capabilities shall be tested, reviewed and improved regularly.

---

## Business Continuity Framework

The enterprise Business Continuity Framework consists of:

- Business Impact Analysis
- Risk Assessment
- Continuity Planning
- Crisis Management
- Operational Recovery
- Disaster Recovery Coordination
- Continuity Testing
- Continuous Improvement

---

## Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

Business Impact Analysis shall identify:

- Critical Business Services
- Critical Business Processes
- Maximum Acceptable Downtime
- Recovery Priorities
- Operational Dependencies
- Customer Impact
- Regulatory Impact
- Financial Impact

Business Impact Analysis shall be reviewed periodically.

---

## Critical Service Identification

Critical services shall be classified according to:

- Business Criticality
- Customer Impact
- Financial Impact
- Regulatory Obligations
- Operational Dependencies
- Recovery Complexity

---

## Continuity Planning

Business Continuity Plans (BCPs) shall define:

- Critical Processes
- Continuity Procedures
- Alternate Operating Methods
- Resource Requirements
- Recovery Teams
- Communication Plans
- Escalation Procedures
- Recovery Validation

Each critical business function shall maintain an approved and current BCP.

---

## Crisis Management

Crisis management shall include:

- Crisis Declaration
- Crisis Leadership
- Decision Coordination
- Stakeholder Communication
- Business Prioritisation
- Recovery Oversight
- Executive Reporting

A designated Crisis Management Team shall coordinate enterprise-wide responses.

---

## Recovery Priorities

Recovery activities shall prioritise:

1. Protection of People
2. Regulatory Obligations
3. Critical Business Services
4. Customer-facing Operations
5. Core Technology Platforms
6. Supporting Business Functions
7. Non-critical Services

---

## Recovery Objectives

Continuity planning shall define:

- Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
- Minimum Business Continuity Requirements
- Service Restoration Priorities

Recovery objectives shall align with business impact assessments.

---

## Third-Party Continuity

Critical suppliers and service providers shall:

- Maintain Business Continuity Plans
- Demonstrate Disaster Recovery capabilities
- Meet agreed recovery objectives
- Participate in continuity reviews where appropriate

Third-party continuity risks shall be periodically assessed.

---

## Communication Management

Business continuity communication shall include:

- Crisis Notification
- Stakeholder Updates
- Customer Communication
- Executive Reporting
- Regulatory Notifications (where required)
- Recovery Status
- Business Resumption Confirmation

Communication shall be accurate, timely and coordinated.

---

## Continuity Testing

Business Continuity Plans shall be validated through:

- Tabletop Exercises
- Simulation Exercises
- Recovery Drills
- Failover Testing
- Communication Testing
- Crisis Response Exercises

Test outcomes shall be documented and improvement actions tracked.

---

## Business Continuity Metrics

Monitor:

- Continuity Plan Coverage
- Continuity Test Success Rate
- Recovery Time Achievement
- Recovery Point Achievement
- Critical Service Availability
- Crisis Response Time
- Business Downtime
- Recovery Readiness
- Continuity Audit Findings

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

Business Continuity teams shall:

- Develop continuity strategies.
- Maintain Business Continuity Plans.
- Coordinate continuity testing.
- Support crisis management.
- Validate operational readiness.
- Review recovery performance.
- Drive continual resilience improvements.

## Compliance

All business-critical functions shall maintain documented, approved and regularly tested Business Continuity Plans.

Business continuity activities shall comply with applicable regulatory, contractual and organisational governance requirements.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Business Continuity shall continuously improve through:

- Business Impact Reviews
- Continuity Testing
- Crisis Exercises
- Incident Learnings
- Risk Assessments
- Regulatory Updates
- Operational Reviews

---

## Final Principle

Business Continuity ensures that the enterprise can sustain critical operations, protect customers, support employees and recover efficiently from disruption through proactive planning, coordinated response and continuous organisational resilience.
