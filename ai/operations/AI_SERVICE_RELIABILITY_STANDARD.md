# AI_SERVICE_RELIABILITY_STANDARD

## Purpose

Establish the enterprise standard for ensuring that business services remain reliable, available, performant, scalable and resilient throughout their operational lifecycle.

This standard defines how service reliability shall be engineered, measured, monitored and continuously improved to ensure uninterrupted business operations.

---

## Objectives

- Establish enterprise service reliability practices.
- Define service reliability requirements.
- Standardise reliability engineering.
- Ensure high service availability.
- Improve operational resilience.
- Reduce service disruptions.
- Standardise performance management.
- Govern scalability and capacity.
- Measure service reliability using objective indicators.
- Drive continuous reliability improvement.

---

## Scope

This standard applies to:

- Business Services
- Enterprise Applications
- APIs
- Microservices
- Cloud Platforms
- AI Services
- Data Services
- Integration Services
- Customer-facing Platforms
- Internal Business Platforms

---

## Service Reliability Principles

### Reliability by Design

Reliability shall be designed into every service rather than added after deployment.

---

### Customer-Centric Reliability

Service reliability shall be measured based on customer experience and business outcomes.

---

### Failure Tolerance

Enterprise services shall tolerate failures without significant business disruption.

---

### Resilience First

Services shall recover gracefully from partial failures.

---

### Continuous Improvement

Reliability shall improve continuously through measurement, review and optimisation.

---

## Service Reliability Model

Service reliability consists of the following capabilities.

### Availability

Ensure services remain accessible.

Includes:

- Uptime
- Service Reachability
- Dependency Availability
- High Availability

---

### Performance

Ensure services respond efficiently.

Includes:

- Response Time
- Throughput
- Latency
- Resource Utilisation

---

### Scalability

Ensure services support increasing workloads.

Includes:

- Horizontal Scaling
- Vertical Scaling
- Auto Scaling
- Load Distribution

---

### Resilience

Ensure services continue operating despite failures.

Includes:

- Retry Logic
- Circuit Breakers
- Graceful Degradation
- Failover
- Self Healing

---

### Capacity

Ensure sufficient resources exist to meet demand.

Includes:

- Capacity Planning
- Resource Forecasting
- Peak Load Planning
- Growth Planning

---

## Service Level Management

### Service Level Indicators (SLIs)

Measure operational performance.

Examples:

- Availability
- Response Time
- Error Rate
- Throughput
- Success Rate

---

### Service Level Objectives (SLOs)

Define target reliability goals.

Examples:

- API Availability
- Transaction Success
- Response Time Targets
- Workflow Completion Rate

---

### Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

Define agreed service commitments with business stakeholders.

---

### Error Budgets

Error budgets shall balance innovation with operational stability.

Error budget consumption shall influence deployment and change decisions.

---

## Reliability Engineering

Enterprise reliability engineering shall include:

- Fault Tolerance
- Load Balancing
- Auto Recovery
- Auto Scaling
- Health Checks
- Dependency Isolation
- Performance Optimisation

## Capacity Management

Capacity planning shall consider:

- User Growth
- Transaction Growth
- Data Growth

## Performance Management

Performance monitoring shall include:

- Response Time

- Concurrent Users
- Resource Usage
- Database Performance
- API Performance

---

## Resilience Management

Enterprise resilience shall include:

- Backup Services
- Redundancy
- Multi-zone Deployment
- Disaster Readiness
- Dependency Recovery
- Automatic Failover

---

## Reliability Monitoring

Reliability shall be continuously monitored using:

- Availability Metrics
- Error Rates
- Performance Metrics
- Capacity Metrics
- Service Health
- AI Service Health

## Reliability Reviews

Regular reviews shall evaluate:

- SLA Compliance
- SLO Achievement
- Availability Trends
- Capacity Trends
- Performance Trends
- Reliability Risks

## Reliability KPIs

Monitor:

- Service Availability

- Uptime
- Response Time
- Error Rate
- Success Rate
- SLA Compliance
- SLO Compliance
