# AI_RUNTIME_OPERATIONS_STANDARD

## Purpose

Establish the enterprise standard for operating production environments by defining how runtime systems, business services, AI capabilities, operational workloads and enterprise platforms shall be monitored, validated, operated and maintained throughout their lifecycle.

This standard ensures that the enterprise remains operational, stable, secure and continuously available.

---

## Objectives

- Standardise day-to-day production operations.
- Define runtime operational procedures.
- Govern production health verification.
- Ensure continuous service availability.
- Standardise runtime validation.
- Define operational readiness requirements.
- Ensure business workflow continuity.
- Govern AI runtime operations.
- Standardise operational verification.
- Establish runtime operational evidence.
- Reduce operational failures through proactive monitoring.

---

This standard applies to:

## Runtime Operations Principles

### Continuous Operations

Enterprise services shall remain continuously operational with minimal disruption.

### Proactive Monitoring

Runtime issues shall be identified before they impact business operations.

### Business-Centric Operations

Operational success shall be measured by successful business outcomes rather than infrastructure status alone.

### Automation First

Routine operational activities should be automated wherever practical.

### Evidence-Based Validation

Operational status shall always be supported by measurable runtime evidence.

### Operational Consistency

Runtime activities shall follow standardised enterprise operating procedures.
