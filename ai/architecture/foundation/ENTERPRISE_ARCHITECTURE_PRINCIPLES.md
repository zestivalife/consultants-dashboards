# Enterprise Architecture Principles

## Principles

1.  Business before implementation.
2.  Single Source of Truth.
3.  Configuration over hardcoded logic.
4.  API-first architecture.
5.  Security by design.
6.  Evidence-based engineering.
7.  Documentation-driven development.
8.  Backward compatibility by default.
9.  Enterprise scalability.
10. Observability and auditability.
