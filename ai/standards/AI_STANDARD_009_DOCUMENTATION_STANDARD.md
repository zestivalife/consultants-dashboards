# Purpose

The Enterprise AI Documentation Standard establishes the mandatory principles, governance, structure, lifecycle and quality requirements for all documentation produced within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Documentation SHALL be treated as an enterprise asset and maintained with the same discipline as software. Every architectural decision, engineering implementation, governance policy, operational procedure and AI capability SHALL be accurately documented, version-controlled and continuously maintained.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise AI Documentation Standard SHALL:

- Standardise enterprise documentation.
- Improve organisational knowledge sharing.
- Ensure documentation consistency.
- Support engineering productivity.
- Improve onboarding efficiency.
- Enable enterprise governance.
- Preserve architectural knowledge.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Improve audit readiness.
- Enable AI-assisted knowledge discovery.

# Documentation Principles

## Principle 1 — Documentation as Code

Documentation SHALL be maintained within version-controlled repositories.

---

## Principle 2 — Single Source of Truth

Every document SHALL have one authoritative version.

---

## Principle 3 — Living Documentation

Documentation SHALL evolve alongside implementation.

---

## Principle 4 — Discoverability

Documentation SHALL be searchable and categorised.

---

## Principle 5 — Traceability

Documentation SHALL reference related artefacts.

---

## Principle 6 — Consistency

Standard templates SHALL be used across the enterprise.

---

## Principle 7 — Accuracy

Documentation SHALL reflect the current implementation.

---

## Principle 8 — Reviewability

Documentation SHALL undergo peer review.

---

## Principle 9 — Accessibility

Documentation SHALL be readable by both humans and AI systems.

---

## Principle 10 — Governance

Documentation SHALL comply with enterprise governance policies.

- Mandatory sections are missing.
- Version information is incomplete.
- References are invalid.
- Governance approvals are absent.
- Traceability is incomplete.
- Templates are not followed.
- Review evidence is unavailable.
