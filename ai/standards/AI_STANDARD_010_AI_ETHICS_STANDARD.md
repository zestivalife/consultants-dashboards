# Purpose

The Enterprise AI Ethics Standard establishes the mandatory ethical principles, governance framework, operational controls and accountability mechanisms governing the design, development, deployment and operation of Artificial Intelligence throughout the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The objective is to ensure all AI capabilities are trustworthy, fair, transparent, accountable and aligned with organisational values, regulatory expectations and societal interests.

Ethics SHALL be embedded into every phase of the AI lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise AI Ethics Standard SHALL:

- Establish enterprise AI ethical principles.
- Ensure responsible AI development.
- Promote fairness and equity.
- Reduce algorithmic bias.
- Increase transparency.
- Enable explainability.
- Protect human rights.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Improve stakeholder trust.
- Support regulatory compliance.

# Ethical Principles

## Principle 1 — Human-Centric AI

AI SHALL augment human capability rather than replace human judgement for critical decisions.

---

## Principle 2 — Fairness

AI systems SHALL minimise unfair bias and discriminatory outcomes.

---

## Principle 3 — Transparency

AI behaviour SHALL be understandable to stakeholders.

---

## Principle 4 — Explainability

Enterprise AI SHALL provide appropriate explanations for significant outputs and decisions.

---

## Principle 5 — Accountability

Every AI capability SHALL have accountable business and technical owners.

---

## Principle 6 — Privacy

Personal information SHALL be protected throughout the AI lifecycle.

---

## Principle 7 — Safety

AI systems SHALL operate within approved safety boundaries.

---

## Principle 8 — Reliability

AI outputs SHALL be continuously evaluated for quality and consistency.

---

## Principle 9 — Human Oversight

Appropriate human review SHALL exist for high-impact decisions.

---

## Principle 10 — Continuous Improvement

Ethical performance SHALL be continuously monitored and improved.

# Ethical Risk Categories

The enterprise SHALL assess:

- Bias Risk
- Privacy Risk
- Safety Risk
- Security Risk
- Manipulation Risk
- Hallucination Risk
- Misinformation Risk
- Regulatory Risk
- Operational Risk
- Reputational Risk

---

# Fairness Requirements

Enterprise AI SHALL:

- Evaluate demographic bias.
- Assess outcome parity.
- Validate representative datasets.
- Monitor fairness metrics.
- Review model behaviour regularly.
- Document mitigation strategies.

---

# Transparency Requirements

Every AI capability SHALL document:

- Business Purpose
- Intended Users
- Decision Boundaries
- Data Sources
- Model Limitations
- Known Risks
- Governance Owner
- Review History

---

# Explainability

Enterprise AI SHALL provide:

- Decision Reasoning
- Confidence Indicators
- Supporting Evidence
- Data Provenance
- Source Attribution
- Human Review Guidance

---

# Human Oversight

Human approval SHALL be mandatory for:

- Medical recommendations
- Financial decisions
- Legal decisions
- Employment decisions
- Regulatory actions
- High-risk automated actions
- Policy exceptions
- Ethical override requests

# Quality Gates

Ethics approval SHALL fail if:

- Ethical assessment is incomplete.
- Fairness evaluation is missing.
- Explainability requirements are unmet.
- Human oversight is undefined.
- Bias mitigation is insufficient.
- Privacy controls are inadequate.
- Regulatory obligations are not satisfied.
