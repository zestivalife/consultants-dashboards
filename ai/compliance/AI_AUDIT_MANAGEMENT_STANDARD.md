# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for planning, conducting, documenting, reporting and continuously improving internal and external audits across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Its objective is to ensure that governance controls, regulatory obligations, enterprise policies and operational processes are independently assessed for effectiveness, compliance and continual improvement.

Governance authority, audit ownership, waiver processes and escalation responsibilities are defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Standardise enterprise audit practices.
- Verify governance effectiveness.
- Assess regulatory compliance.
- Identify control weaknesses.
- Improve organisational transparency.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Support AI governance assurance.
- Enable evidence-based improvement.
- Maintain audit readiness.

# 4. Audit Principles

## 4.1 Independence

Audits shall be conducted objectively and independently from the activities being audited.

---

## 4.2 Evidence-Based Assessment

Audit conclusions shall be based on objective, verifiable and documented evidence.

---

## 4.3 Risk-Based Prioritisation

Audit planning shall prioritise areas presenting the greatest business, regulatory, operational or security risk.

---

## 4.4 Transparency

Audit findings, recommendations and corrective actions shall be documented and communicated to appropriate stakeholders.

---

## 4.5 Continuous Readiness

Audit readiness shall be maintained continuously rather than prepared only before scheduled audits.

---

## 4.6 AI Auditability

AI systems shall maintain sufficient evidence, traceability and operational transparency to support effective auditing.

---

# 5. Audit Types

The enterprise shall support multiple audit categories, including:

## Internal Audits

Evaluate compliance with enterprise governance, policies and standards.

---

## External Audits

Conducted by customers, regulators, certification bodies or independent third parties.

---

## Compliance Audits

Verify adherence to legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

---

## Security Audits

Assess implementation and effectiveness of security controls.

---

## Operational Audits

Evaluate operational efficiency, reliability and governance maturity.

---

## AI Governance Audits

Assess AI governance, model oversight, responsible AI practices, human review processes and AI operational controls.

---

# 6. Audit Lifecycle

Every audit shall follow the defined lifecycle:

1. Audit Planning
2. Scope Definition
3. Risk Assessment
4. Evidence Collection
5. Control Evaluation
6. Finding Documentation
7. Management Review
8. Corrective Action Planning
9. Verification
10. Audit Closure

Each stage shall be documented.

---

# 7. Audit Planning

Audit planning shall identify:

- Audit Objectives
- Audit Scope
- Applicable Standards
- Regulatory Requirements
- Audit Criteria
- Audit Schedule
- Required Resources
- Stakeholders
- Reporting Requirements

Risk assessments shall influence audit frequency and scope.

---

# 8. AI Audit Requirements

Where AI capabilities are implemented, audits shall additionally evaluate:

- AI Governance Compliance
- Human Oversight
- Prompt Management
- Model Monitoring
- Explainability
- Decision Traceability
- AI Risk Controls
- Responsible AI Practices
- Audit Log Integrity

AI systems shall provide sufficient evidence to reconstruct significant operational decisions where appropriate.

---

# 9. Audit Controls

Mandatory audit controls include:

- Approved Audit Plans
- Evidence Collection Procedures
- Auditor Independence
- Audit Working Papers
- Finding Classification
- Corrective Action Tracking
- Audit Trail Maintenance
- Management Review

Audit evidence shall remain protected from unauthorised modification.

---

# 10. Audit Evidence

Audit evidence may include:

- Governance Documents
- Policies
- Standards
- Process Documentation
- System Logs
- Audit Logs
- Configuration Records
- Test Results
- Risk Assessments
- AI Operational Evidence
- Corrective Action Records

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

---

# 11. Metrics

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- Audit Completion Rate
- Audit Findings
- Critical Findings
- Corrective Action Completion Rate
- Repeat Findings
- Audit Readiness Score
- Compliance Coverage
- AI Governance Audit Score
- Average Audit Duration
- Outstanding Audit Actions

# 12. Roles & Responsibilities

### Audit Team

- Plan and conduct audits.
- Collect objective evidence.
- Document findings.
- Verify corrective actions.

---

### Process Owners

- Support audit activities.
- Provide requested evidence.
- Implement corrective actions.
- Participate in audit reviews.

---

### Compliance Team

- Coordinate enterprise audits.
- Maintain audit schedules.
- Monitor corrective actions.
- Report audit outcomes.

---

### Compliance Domain Owner

- Govern enterprise audit activities.
- Approve audit programmes.
- Review significant findings.
- Escalate enterprise risks where necessary.

---

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise audit activities governed by EAIOS.

Audit findings shall be addressed within agreed timeframes and supported by documented corrective actions.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

Audit Management shall continuously improve through:

- Audit Trend Analysis
- Lessons Learned
- Root Cause Analysis
- Regulatory Updates
- Governance Reviews
- AI Governance Improvements
- Industry Best Practices
