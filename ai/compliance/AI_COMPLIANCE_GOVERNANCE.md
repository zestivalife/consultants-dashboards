# 1. Purpose

This governance document establishes the enterprise framework for ensuring that all organisational activities comply with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, organisational and ethical obligations.

Compliance Governance provides the policies, authority, accountability and oversight necessary to ensure that compliance is embedded throughout the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Implementation requirements are defined within the Compliance Standards that inherit from this governance document.

---

# 2. Governance Authority

The Enterprise Governance Board is the ultimate authority for Compliance Governance.

The Compliance Domain Owner is responsible for implementing, monitoring and continuously improving the enterprise compliance framework.

No Compliance Standard may contradict this governance without formal approval from the Enterprise Governance Board.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

The Compliance Governance framework shall operate according to the following principles:

- Compliance by Design
- Risk-Based Governance
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Evidence-Based Decisions
- Continuous Monitoring
- Regulatory Adaptability
- Ethical AI Usage
- Continuous Improvement

# 6. Decision Rights

The Compliance Domain Owner has authority to:

- Define enterprise compliance policies.
- Require compliance assessments.
- Initiate compliance audits.
- Escalate non-compliance.
- Recommend corrective actions.

The Enterprise Governance Board retains authority to:

- Approve governance changes.
- Approve enterprise waivers.
- Accept significant compliance risks.
- Resolve governance disputes.

---

# 7. Governance Model

Compliance Governance shall operate through the following lifecycle:

1. Identify Obligations
2. Interpret Requirements
3. Define Controls
4. Implement Controls
5. Monitor Compliance
6. Audit Compliance
7. Report Findings
8. Resolve Non-Compliance
9. Improve Governance

---

# 8. Governance Policies

The enterprise shall:

- Maintain documented compliance obligations.
- Embed compliance into every SDLC phase.
- Maintain evidence supporting compliance.
- Perform periodic compliance assessments.
- Conduct internal audits.
- Monitor regulatory changes.
- Maintain corrective action plans.
- Ensure AI systems comply with organisational governance.

Compliance shall be proactive rather than reactive.

---

# 9. Exception & Waiver Process

Compliance exceptions shall:

- Be documented.
- Include business justification.
- Assess associated risks.
- Define compensating controls.
- Include approval authority.
- Specify review and expiry dates.

Permanent waivers are prohibited.

---

# 10. Escalation Path

Compliance issues shall be escalated according to severity:

1. Team Lead
2. Domain Owner
3. Enterprise Governance Board
4. Executive Leadership

Critical regulatory breaches shall be escalated immediately.

---

# 11. Roles & Responsibilities

### Compliance Domain Owner

- Govern enterprise compliance.
- Interpret regulatory requirements.
- Coordinate audits.
- Report compliance status.

### Engineering Teams

- Implement compliant solutions.
- Maintain required evidence.
- Address compliance findings.

### Security Teams

- Support compliance controls.
- Validate security-related obligations.

### Executive Governance Board

- Provide strategic oversight.
- Approve governance updates.
- Review enterprise compliance posture.

---

# 12. Governance Metrics & Reporting

Governance reporting shall include:

- Compliance Score
- Audit Completion Rate
- Regulatory Findings
- Non-Compliance Rate
- Corrective Action Completion
- Waiver Count
- Open Compliance Risks
- AI Compliance Assessments

Reports shall be reviewed quarterly.
