# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for creating, reviewing, approving, publishing, maintaining and retiring organisational policies and governance documents.

Its objective is to ensure that enterprise documentation remains accurate, consistent, controlled and aligned with organisational objectives, regulatory obligations and governance requirements.

Governance authority, policy ownership, approval responsibilities and waiver processes are defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Standardise enterprise policy management.
- Ensure consistent governance documentation.
- Maintain document integrity.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Improve policy accessibility.
- Ensure controlled document lifecycle management.
- Enable traceability of governance decisions.
- Support AI governance documentation.
- Promote continuous governance maturity.

# 4. Policy Management Principles

## 4.1 Single Source of Truth

Every enterprise policy shall exist as one authoritative, approved version.

---

## 4.2 Governance Before Implementation

Policies define governance requirements.

Standards and procedures define implementation requirements.

---

## 4.3 Controlled Lifecycle

Every policy shall follow a controlled lifecycle from creation through retirement.

---

## 4.4 Traceability

Every policy shall maintain complete version history, approval records and change justification.

---

## 4.5 Accessibility

Approved policies shall be readily accessible to authorised personnel.

---

## 4.6 AI Documentation Governance

AI-generated documentation may assist policy development but shall undergo human review and formal approval before publication.

---

# 5. Policy Lifecycle

Every enterprise policy shall progress through the following lifecycle:

1. Identify Need
2. Draft
3. Review
4. Stakeholder Consultation
5. Approval
6. Publication
7. Communication
8. Implementation
9. Periodic Review
10. Revision or Retirement

Each lifecycle stage shall be documented.

---

# 6. Policy Structure

Enterprise policies shall include, where applicable:

- Metadata
- Purpose
- Scope
- Governance Authority
- Roles & Responsibilities
- Policies
- Controls
- Compliance Requirements
- Review Schedule
- Related Documents
- Version History

A consistent enterprise document structure shall be maintained across governance artefacts.

---

# 7. Document Control

All controlled documents shall maintain:

- Unique Document Identifier
- Version Number
- Approval Authority
- Effective Date
- Review Date
- Classification
- Document Owner
- Change History
- Status

Unauthorised document modification is prohibited.

---

# 8. AI Policy Requirements

Policies governing AI systems shall additionally address:

- Human Oversight
- Responsible AI
- Explainability
- Accountability
- Data Governance
- Security
- Risk Management
- Ethical Use
- Regulatory Compliance

AI governance documentation shall remain aligned with enterprise AI governance objectives.

---

# 9. Policy Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Document Approval Workflow
- Version Control
- Access Control
- Change Management
- Periodic Review
- Publication Control
- Retirement Process
- Audit Trail

Policy changes shall not become effective until formally approved.

# 10. Policy Evidence

Evidence supporting policy management shall include:

- Approved Documents

- Approval Records
- Change Logs
- Version History
- Distribution Records
- Acknowledgement Records
- Retirement Records

Evidence shall support governance audits and regulatory compliance.
