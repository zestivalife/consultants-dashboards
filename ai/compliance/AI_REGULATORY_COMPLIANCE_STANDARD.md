# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for identifying, interpreting, implementing and maintaining compliance with all applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

Its objective is to ensure that enterprise systems remain compliant throughout their lifecycle while adapting to evolving regulatory environments without disrupting business operations.

Governance authority, policy ownership, waiver processes and accountability are defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Identify applicable regulatory obligations.
- Ensure regulatory compliance throughout the SDLC.
- Reduce regulatory risk.
- Standardise compliance implementation.
- Support regulatory audits.
- Protect organisational reputation.
- Ensure responsible AI deployment.
- Maintain compliance evidence.
- Enable continuous regulatory readiness.

# 4. Regulatory Compliance Principles

## 4.1 Compliance by Design

Regulatory requirements shall be incorporated during planning, architecture, development and operations rather than addressed after implementation.

---

## 4.2 Continuous Compliance

Compliance shall be continuously monitored throughout the product lifecycle.

---

## 4.3 Risk-Based Implementation

Compliance activities shall prioritise obligations that present the greatest business, legal or customer risk.

---

## 4.4 Traceability

Every regulatory requirement shall be traceable to implemented controls, supporting evidence and verification activities.

---

## 4.5 Accountability

Compliance responsibilities shall be clearly assigned and documented.

---

## 4.6 AI Regulatory Responsibility

AI systems shall comply with all applicable organisational, contractual and regulatory obligations, including requirements related to transparency, accountability and human oversight.

---

# 5. Regulatory Identification

The enterprise shall maintain an inventory of applicable:

- Laws
- Regulations
- Industry Standards
- Contractual Obligations
- Customer Requirements
- Internal Policies

Each obligation shall identify:

- Source
- Applicability
- Business Impact
- Responsible Owner
- Required Controls

---

# 6. Compliance Implementation

Regulatory requirements shall be translated into:

- Enterprise Policies
- Engineering Standards
- Security Controls
- Operational Procedures
- Quality Requirements
- Audit Activities
- Training Requirements

Implementation shall be documented and approved.

---

# 7. Regulatory Change Management

The organisation shall monitor changes to:

- Laws
- Regulations
- Industry Standards
- Regulatory Guidance
- AI Governance Frameworks
- Customer Contractual Requirements

Regulatory changes shall be:

1. Assessed
2. Prioritised
3. Assigned
4. Implemented
5. Verified
6. Communicated

---

# 8. AI Regulatory Requirements

Where AI capabilities are implemented, compliance activities shall additionally evaluate:

- Human Oversight
- Transparency
- Explainability
- Accountability
- Data Governance
- Bias Management
- Model Monitoring
- Auditability
- Responsible AI Practices

AI-related regulatory obligations shall be incorporated into enterprise engineering and operational controls.

---

# 9. Compliance Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Regulatory Register
- Compliance Mapping
- Control Assessments
- Compliance Reviews
- Internal Audits
- Change Monitoring
- Documentation Reviews
- Management Reporting

Controls shall be periodically reviewed for effectiveness.

---

# 10. Compliance Evidence

Compliance evidence shall include:

- Regulatory Register
- Compliance Assessments
- Control Documentation
- Audit Reports
- Training Records
- Risk Assessments
- AI Compliance Assessments
- Corrective Action Records

Evidence shall be retained according to enterprise record retention requirements.

---

# 11. Metrics

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- Compliance Coverage
- Regulatory Findings
- Open Compliance Risks
- Regulatory Changes Implemented
- Audit Observation Rate
- Corrective Action Completion Rate
- Compliance Training Completion
- AI Regulatory Compliance Score
- Non-Compliance Incidents
- Regulatory Response Time

# 12. Roles & Responsibilities

### Compliance Team

- Interpret regulatory requirements.
- Maintain the regulatory register.
- Coordinate compliance activities.
- Report compliance status.

---

### Engineering Teams

- Implement required controls.
- Maintain implementation evidence.
- Resolve compliance findings.

---

### Security Teams

- Implement regulatory security controls.
- Support compliance assessments.
- Monitor regulatory security risks.

---

### Product Owners

- Ensure product requirements support regulatory obligations.
- Prioritise regulatory implementation activities.

---

### Compliance Domain Owner

- Govern enterprise regulatory compliance.
- Review compliance effectiveness.
- Approve compliance implementation guidance.
- Escalate significant compliance risks.

---

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise systems governed by EAIOS.

Applicable regulatory requirements shall be implemented, monitored and evidenced throughout the system lifecycle.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.
