# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for continuously monitoring compliance with organisational policies, regulatory obligations, governance controls and enterprise standards.

Its objective is to ensure that compliance is maintained proactively through continuous observation, measurement, reporting and corrective action rather than relying solely on periodic audits.

Governance authority, approval responsibilities, waivers and escalation procedures are defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Establish continuous compliance monitoring.
- Detect compliance deviations early.
- Improve governance visibility.
- Reduce regulatory risk.
- Support audit readiness.
- Enable proactive remediation.
- Strengthen AI governance oversight.
- Improve organisational accountability.
- Drive continuous compliance improvement.

# 4. Compliance Monitoring Principles

## 4.1 Continuous Visibility

Compliance shall be monitored continuously throughout the operational lifecycle.

---

## 4.2 Risk-Based Monitoring

Monitoring activities shall prioritise areas with the greatest business, regulatory and operational risk.

---

## 4.3 Evidence-Based Assessment

Compliance status shall be determined using objective, measurable and verifiable evidence.

---

## 4.4 Timely Detection

Compliance deviations shall be identified and reported as early as possible.

---

## 4.5 Transparency

Compliance results shall be visible to authorised stakeholders through structured reporting and dashboards.

---

## 4.6 AI Governance Monitoring

AI systems shall be continuously monitored to ensure ongoing compliance with enterprise governance, ethical principles and applicable regulatory obligations.

---

# 5. Monitoring Framework

Enterprise compliance monitoring shall include:

- Regulatory Compliance
- Governance Compliance
- Policy Compliance
- Security Compliance
- Operational Compliance
- Quality Compliance
- AI Governance Compliance
- Third-party Compliance

Monitoring shall be integrated into normal business operations wherever practical.

---

# 6. Monitoring Activities

Monitoring activities may include:

- Automated Compliance Checks
- Manual Reviews
- Dashboard Monitoring
- KPI Reviews
- Exception Reviews
- Configuration Validation
- Control Effectiveness Reviews
- Documentation Verification
- Management Reporting

Monitoring frequency shall reflect organisational risk.

---

# 7. Compliance Indicators

The enterprise shall monitor indicators including:

- Policy Compliance
- Control Compliance
- Regulatory Compliance
- Audit Findings
- Outstanding Risks
- Corrective Actions
- Waivers
- Training Completion
- AI Governance Compliance

Thresholds shall be defined for significant compliance deviations requiring escalation.

---

# 8. AI Compliance Requirements

Where AI capabilities are implemented, monitoring shall additionally evaluate:

- Responsible AI Compliance
- Human Oversight
- Prompt Governance
- Model Behaviour
- AI Decision Traceability
- AI Risk Controls
- AI Audit Logs
- AI Operational Monitoring
- Ethical AI Compliance

Monitoring shall verify that AI systems continue operating within approved governance boundaries.

---

# 9. Monitoring Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Compliance Dashboards
- KPI Monitoring
- Automated Alerts
- Exception Registers
- Compliance Reviews
- Escalation Procedures
- Evidence Collection
- Trend Analysis

Monitoring controls shall be periodically reviewed for effectiveness.

---

# 10. Compliance Evidence

Evidence supporting monitoring activities shall include:

- Compliance Dashboards
- KPI Reports
- Monitoring Logs
- Exception Registers
- Risk Reports
- Compliance Assessments
- AI Monitoring Reports
- Corrective Action Status Reports
- Management Review Records

Evidence shall support audits, governance reviews and regulatory reporting.

This standard defines the mandatory enterprise requirements for continuous compliance monitoring across all products, platforms, AI systems and business operations governed by the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).
