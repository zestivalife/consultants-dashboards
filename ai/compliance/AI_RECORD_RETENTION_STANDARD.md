# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for the creation, classification, retention, protection, archival and disposal of organisational records.

Its objective is to ensure that records remain available, accurate, secure and compliant throughout their lifecycle while supporting legal, regulatory, operational and business requirements.

Governance authority, ownership, waiver processes and accountability are defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Standardise enterprise record management.
- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Maintain record integrity.
- Support audit readiness.
- Protect business evidence.
- Enable secure archival.
- Support AI governance traceability.
- Reduce unnecessary data retention.
- Enable controlled record disposal.

# 4. Record Retention Principles

## 4.1 Records are Enterprise Assets

Enterprise records shall be managed as valuable organisational assets throughout their lifecycle.

---

## 4.2 Retention by Obligation

Retention periods shall be determined by applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and business requirements.

---

## 4.3 Integrity

Records shall remain complete, accurate and protected from unauthorised modification throughout their retention period.

---

## 4.4 Accessibility

Authorised users shall be able to retrieve retained records within defined operational timeframes.

---

## 4.5 Secure Disposal

Records shall be securely destroyed when retention obligations expire, unless legal or regulatory requirements mandate continued preservation.

---

## 4.6 AI Record Traceability

AI systems shall maintain sufficient records to support transparency, governance, auditability and operational accountability.

---

# 5. Record Lifecycle

Every enterprise record shall progress through the following lifecycle:

1. Creation
2. Classification
3. Storage
4. Protection
5. Access
6. Retention
7. Archive
8. Retrieval
9. Disposal

Each stage shall be governed by documented controls.

---

# 6. Record Classification

Enterprise records shall be classified according to:

- Business Value
- Confidentiality
- Regulatory Requirements
- Operational Importance
- Security Classification
- Retention Requirements

Classification shall determine applicable handling and retention controls.

---

# 7. Retention Requirements

Each record category shall define:

- Record Owner
- Classification
- Retention Period
- Storage Location
- Protection Controls
- Disposal Method
- Review Frequency

Retention schedules shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued regulatory alignment.

---

# 8. AI Record Requirements

Where AI capabilities are implemented, retained records shall include, where applicable:

- AI Model Versions
- Prompt Templates
- Prompt Revision History
- Model Evaluation Results
- Human Review Records
- AI Decision Logs
- AI Risk Assessments
- AI Audit Evidence
- AI Governance Approvals

AI records shall support explainability, traceability and governance.

---

# 9. Record Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Record Classification
- Version Control
- Access Control
- Encryption (where applicable)
- Backup and Recovery
- Integrity Validation
- Retention Monitoring
- Secure Disposal
- Audit Logging

Unauthorised deletion or modification of enterprise records is prohibited.

---

# 10. Record Evidence

Evidence supporting record management shall include:

- Record Inventories
- Retention Schedules
- Archive Registers
- Disposal Records
- Backup Verification Reports
- Access Logs
- Audit Logs
- AI Governance Records
- Record Review Reports

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise governance and regulatory requirements.

---

# 11. Metrics

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- Record Classification Coverage

- Record Retrieval Success Rate
- Archive Completion Rate
- Disposal Compliance Rate

- Backup Verification Success Rate

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise records governed by EAIOS.

Records shall be retained, protected and disposed of in accordance with approved retention schedules and applicable regulatory requirements.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.
