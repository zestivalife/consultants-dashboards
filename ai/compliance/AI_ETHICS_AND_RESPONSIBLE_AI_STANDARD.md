# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for the ethical design, development, deployment, operation and governance of Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems.

Its objective is to ensure that AI capabilities are trustworthy, transparent, accountable and aligned with organisational values, legal obligations and responsible business practices.

Governance authority, approval responsibilities, waiver processes and accountability are defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Promote responsible AI development.
- Protect human rights and dignity.
- Reduce ethical and regulatory risks.
- Ensure transparency and accountability.
- Encourage fair and unbiased AI outcomes.
- Strengthen human oversight.
- Increase stakeholder trust.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable continuous ethical improvement.

# 4. Responsible AI Principles

## 4.1 Human-Centred AI

AI shall enhance human capability and decision-making rather than replace human accountability.

---

## 4.2 Human Oversight

Appropriate human review and intervention shall be available for AI-assisted decisions according to business risk.

---

## 4.3 Fairness

AI systems shall be designed and monitored to minimise unfair bias and discriminatory outcomes.

---

## 4.4 Transparency

Users and stakeholders shall be informed when interacting with AI where appropriate, and significant AI-assisted decisions shall be explainable to an appropriate level.

---

## 4.5 Accountability

The organisation remains accountable for outcomes produced by AI systems regardless of the underlying technology or provider.

---

## 4.6 Privacy and Security

AI systems shall respect privacy requirements and operate in accordance with enterprise security and data governance standards.

---

## 4.7 Reliability

AI systems shall be validated to ensure consistent, safe and predictable behaviour within approved operating boundaries.

---

## 4.8 Continuous Monitoring

AI systems shall be continuously monitored to identify performance degradation, bias, security issues and governance risks.

---

# 5. Responsible AI Lifecycle

Responsible AI governance shall be integrated throughout the AI lifecycle:

1. Business Need Identification
2. Risk Assessment
3. Data Assessment
4. Model or AI Service Selection
5. Design
6. Development
7. Validation
8. Human Review
9. Deployment
10. Continuous Monitoring
11. Improvement
12. Retirement

Each stage shall maintain documented governance evidence.

---

# 6. Ethical Risk Assessment

Every significant AI implementation shall evaluate:

- Intended Purpose
- Business Impact
- Customer Impact
- Ethical Risks
- Bias Risks
- Privacy Risks
- Security Risks
- Regulatory Risks
- Operational Risks
- Human Oversight Requirements

Risk assessments shall be reviewed periodically.

---

# 7. Responsible AI Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- AI Risk Assessment
- Human Approval Processes
- Prompt Governance
- Model Validation
- Bias Assessment
- Security Validation
- Performance Monitoring
- Audit Logging
- Incident Management
- Version Control

Controls shall be proportionate to the business and regulatory risk presented by the AI system.

---

# 8. Explainability Requirements

Where appropriate, AI systems shall provide:

- Decision Context
- Supporting Evidence
- Confidence Indicators (where available)
- Human Review Pathways
- Decision Traceability
- Model Version Identification

The level of explainability shall be proportionate to the impact of the AI-assisted decision.

---

# 9. AI Governance Evidence

Evidence supporting Responsible AI shall include:

- AI Risk Assessments
- Model Evaluation Reports
- Prompt Reviews
- Human Approval Records
- Bias Assessment Reports
- Validation Results
- Monitoring Reports
- Audit Logs
- Incident Records
- Governance Approvals

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

# 12. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all AI systems governed by EAIOS.

AI systems shall demonstrate responsible design, appropriate governance, effective human oversight and continuous monitoring throughout their operational lifecycle.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

# 15. Standard Statement

This standard defines the mandatory enterprise requirements for Ethical and Responsible AI across all AI systems governed by the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

All AI capabilities shall be designed, implemented, monitored and continuously improved using transparent, accountable and human-centred governance practices that ensure ethical operation, regulatory compliance and organisational trust under the authority established by **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.
