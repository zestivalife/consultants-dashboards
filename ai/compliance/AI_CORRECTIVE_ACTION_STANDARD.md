# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for identifying, analysing, implementing, verifying and monitoring corrective actions arising from audits, compliance findings, security incidents, operational failures, quality issues and AI governance assessments.

The objective is to eliminate the root causes of non-conformities, reduce organisational risk and continuously improve enterprise governance, engineering and operational effectiveness.

Governance authority, ownership, approvals and waiver processes are defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Standardise enterprise corrective action management.
- Eliminate root causes of non-conformities.
- Reduce recurring issues.
- Improve governance effectiveness.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Ensure timely remediation.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Improve AI governance maturity.
- Drive continuous organisational improvement.

# 4. Corrective Action Principles

## 4.1 Root Cause Before Resolution

Corrective actions shall address the underlying cause rather than only the observed issue.

---

## 4.2 Timely Response

Corrective actions shall be initiated and completed within defined timeframes based on business risk and severity.

---

## 4.3 Risk-Based Prioritisation

Corrective actions shall be prioritised according to regulatory, operational, financial, security and customer impact.

---

## 4.4 Accountability

Every corrective action shall have a clearly identified owner responsible for implementation and verification.

---

## 4.5 Evidence-Based Closure

Corrective actions shall not be considered complete until objective evidence demonstrates that the identified issue has been effectively resolved.

---

## 4.6 AI Governance

Corrective actions relating to AI systems shall address governance, model behaviour, human oversight and operational controls in addition to technical remediation.

---

# 5. Corrective Action Lifecycle

Every corrective action shall follow the enterprise lifecycle:

1. Identification
2. Registration
3. Risk Assessment
4. Root Cause Analysis
5. Action Planning
6. Implementation
7. Verification
8. Management Review
9. Closure
10. Effectiveness Monitoring

Each stage shall be documented and traceable.

---

# 6. Root Cause Analysis

Root Cause Analysis (RCA) shall be performed for:

- Critical Audit Findings
- Regulatory Violations
- Security Incidents
- Production Failures
- Major Quality Issues
- Repeated Non-Conformities
- Significant AI Governance Failures

RCA shall identify:

- Immediate Cause
- Root Cause
- Contributing Factors
- Process Weaknesses
- Control Gaps
- Preventive Recommendations

---

# 7. Corrective Action Planning

Every corrective action plan shall define:

- Action Description
- Business Justification
- Risk Rating
- Responsible Owner
- Required Resources
- Target Completion Date
- Verification Method
- Success Criteria
- Escalation Requirements

Action plans shall be approved before implementation.

---

# 8. AI Corrective Action Requirements

Where AI systems are involved, corrective actions shall additionally evaluate:

- Prompt Design
- Model Performance
- Human Review Processes
- AI Guardrails
- Decision Traceability
- AI Monitoring
- Responsible AI Controls
- AI Governance Documentation

Corrective actions shall ensure AI systems continue operating within approved governance boundaries.

---

# 9. Corrective Action Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Corrective Action Register
- Root Cause Analysis
- Risk Assessment
- Approval Workflow
- Progress Tracking
- Effectiveness Verification
- Closure Approval
- Audit Trail

Corrective actions shall remain open until effectiveness has been verified.

---

# 10. Corrective Action Evidence

Evidence supporting corrective actions shall include:

- Corrective Action Register
- Root Cause Analysis Reports
- Action Plans
- Implementation Records
- Verification Reports
- Closure Approvals
- AI Assessment Reports
- Supporting Audit Evidence

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

---

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise corrective actions governed by EAIOS.

Corrective actions shall be documented, risk-assessed, implemented, verified and formally approved before closure.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.

# 16. Standard Statement

This standard defines the mandatory enterprise requirements for corrective action management across all products, platforms, AI systems and business operations governed by the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

All non-conformities shall be systematically analysed, remediated, verified and monitored through a structured, evidence-based corrective action process that eliminates root causes, prevents recurrence and strengthens enterprise governance under the authority established by **AI_COMPLIANCE_GOVERNANCE.md**.
