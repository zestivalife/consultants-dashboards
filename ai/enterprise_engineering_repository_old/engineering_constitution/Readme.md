# Purpose

The Engineering Constitution establishes the non-negotiable engineering principles, governance framework, architectural philosophy, and quality standards governing the development, operation, and evolution of the Zestiva Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Every engineering artefact, document, service, API, workflow, data model, AI capability, infrastructure component, deployment, and operational process SHALL comply with the principles defined within this folder.

This folder serves as the constitutional foundation of the entire Engineering Repository.

---

# Objectives

The Engineering Constitution aims to:

- Define engineering philosophy
- Establish enterprise engineering principles
- Standardise documentation practices
- Ensure architectural consistency
- Govern software quality
- Promote secure-by-design engineering
- Enable scalable AI-native architecture
- Support healthcare and enterprise compliance
- Improve maintainability
- Ensure long-term platform sustainability
