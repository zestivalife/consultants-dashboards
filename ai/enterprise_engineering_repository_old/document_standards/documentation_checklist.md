# 1. Purpose

## 1.1 Objective

This standard defines the mandatory quality assurance checklist that SHALL be completed before any engineering document is approved for publication.

The checklist ensures structural consistency, technical accuracy, traceability, governance compliance and implementation readiness.

---

# 2. Scope

This checklist applies to all engineering documents, including:

- Constitutional Standards
- Engineering Standards
- Architecture Specifications
- Engineering Specifications
- Product Specifications
- API Specifications
- Database Specifications
- Security Specifications
- Operational Specifications
- Testing Specifications

---

# 3. Checklist Principles

Every engineering document SHALL:

- Pass every mandatory checklist item.
- Record review evidence.
- Resolve all Critical and Major findings.
- Be approved before publication.

Documents failing mandatory checks SHALL NOT progress to implementation.

# 11. Publication Checklist

Publication SHALL only occur when:

- All mandatory checklist items are complete.
- No Critical findings remain open.
- No Major findings remain open.
- Approval has been recorded.
- Revision history has been updated.
- Repository references are valid.
