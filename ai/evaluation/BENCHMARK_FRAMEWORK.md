# Purpose

The Enterprise Benchmark Framework establishes the standard architecture, methodology and governance for benchmarking every AI capability across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The Benchmark Framework provides objective, repeatable and measurable methods for assessing prompts, agents, workflows, reasoning engines, knowledge systems and business outcomes against enterprise-defined quality standards.

Every production AI capability SHALL be benchmarked before deployment and continuously throughout its operational lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Benchmark Framework SHALL:

- Standardise benchmark methodologies.
- Enable objective capability comparison.
- Measure AI quality consistently.
- Detect capability degradation.
- Support enterprise governance.
- Enable regression detection.
- Drive continuous optimisation.
- Improve business confidence.
- Support certification.
- Establish enterprise quality baselines.

# Benchmark Principles

## Principle 1 — Repeatability

Benchmarks SHALL produce consistent results under equivalent conditions.

---

## Principle 2 — Fairness

All evaluated capabilities SHALL be tested under identical benchmark conditions.

---

## Principle 3 — Business Alignment

Benchmarks SHALL prioritise measurable business outcomes.

---

## Principle 4 — Comprehensive Coverage

Benchmarks SHALL include functional, operational and governance dimensions.

---

## Principle 5 — Continuous Execution

Benchmarking SHALL occur continuously throughout the AI lifecycle.

---

## Principle 6 — Enterprise Governance

Benchmark execution SHALL comply with enterprise governance policies.

# Quality Gates

Benchmark execution SHALL fail if:

- Benchmark dataset is incomplete.
- Ground truth is unavailable.
- Evaluation metrics are undefined.
- Governance validation fails.
- Required telemetry is unavailable.
- Baseline comparison cannot be performed.
- Audit metadata is incomplete.
