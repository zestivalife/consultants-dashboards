# Purpose

The Enterprise AI Judge Framework establishes the architecture, governance, execution model and scoring methodology for AI-based evaluation within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The AI Judge acts as an independent evaluator capable of objectively assessing prompts, agents, workflows, reasoning quality, business outcomes and governance compliance using predefined evaluation criteria.

Every production AI capability SHALL support AI Judge-based evaluation.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise AI Judge Framework SHALL:

- Standardise AI-based evaluation.
- Reduce manual evaluation effort.
- Ensure objective scoring.
- Enable scalable quality assurance.
- Detect reasoning defects.
- Validate business outcomes.
- Support governance enforcement.
- Improve evaluation consistency.
- Enable autonomous optimisation.
- Support enterprise certification.

# AI Judge Principles

## Principle 1 — Independence

The AI Judge SHALL evaluate independently from the capability under assessment.

---

## Principle 2 — Deterministic Evaluation

Equivalent evaluation inputs SHALL produce equivalent evaluation outcomes.

---

## Principle 3 — Explainability

Every evaluation SHALL include reasoning and justification.

---

## Principle 4 — Evidence-Based Judgement

All evaluation decisions SHALL be supported by measurable evidence.

---

## Principle 5 — Governance First

Evaluation SHALL enforce enterprise governance policies.

---

## Principle 6 — Human Override

Critical evaluation decisions SHALL support human review and override.

# Quality Gates

Evaluation SHALL fail if:

- Required evidence is unavailable.
- Evaluation rubric is undefined.
- Confidence is below threshold.
- Governance validation fails.
- Consensus cannot be reached.
- Audit metadata is incomplete.
- Explainability requirements are not satisfied.
