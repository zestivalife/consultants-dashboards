# Purpose

The Enterprise AI Certification Framework establishes the enterprise-wide governance, architecture and operating model for certifying AI capabilities before they are deployed into production within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Certification provides formal assurance that AI capabilities satisfy enterprise standards for quality, security, governance, compliance, performance and business value.

No production AI capability SHALL be deployed without an approved certification decision.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise AI Certification Framework SHALL:

- Standardise enterprise certification.
- Ensure production readiness.
- Validate governance compliance.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Improve business confidence.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable repeatable certification.
- Maintain enterprise quality standards.
- Provide audit evidence.
- Support continuous re-certification.

# Certification Principles

## Principle 1 — Production Readiness

Only production-ready capabilities SHALL receive certification.

---

## Principle 2 — Evidence-Based Decisions

Certification SHALL rely upon objective evidence.

---

## Principle 3 — Independent Assessment

Certification SHALL be performed independently of implementation teams.

---

## Principle 4 — Enterprise Governance

Certification SHALL verify compliance with enterprise governance.

---

## Principle 5 — Continuous Certification

Certification SHALL continue throughout the operational lifecycle.

---

## Principle 6 — Full Traceability

Every certification decision SHALL be auditable.

# Quality Gates

Certification SHALL fail if:

- Mandatory evidence is missing.
- Benchmark thresholds are not achieved.
- Human evaluation is incomplete.
- Security validation fails.
- Governance approval is missing.
- Operational readiness is not demonstrated.
- Audit evidence is incomplete.
