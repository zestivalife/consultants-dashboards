# Purpose

The Enterprise Evaluation Analytics Framework establishes the enterprise architecture, operating model and governance for analysing evaluation data generated across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The framework transforms evaluation events into actionable intelligence by correlating quality, business outcomes, operational performance, governance compliance and AI behaviour over time.

Evaluation Analytics SHALL serve as the single source of truth for enterprise AI quality intelligence.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Evaluation Analytics Framework SHALL:

- Centralise evaluation analytics.
- Provide enterprise-wide visibility.
- Detect quality trends.
- Correlate technical and business outcomes.
- Predict future quality risks.
- Support executive decision-making.
- Enable continuous optimisation.
- Measure enterprise AI maturity.
- Improve governance oversight.
- Support autonomous quality intelligence.

---

This framework applies to:

Enterprise decisions SHALL be supported by measurable evaluation evidence.

## Principle 2 — Unified Analytics

## Principle 3 — Continuous Intelligence

Analytics SHALL execute continuously across all enterprise AI capabilities.

---

## Principle 4 — Business Alignment

Analytics SHALL prioritise business impact over isolated technical metrics.

---

## Principle 5 — Predictive Insights

---

## Principle 6 — Explainability
