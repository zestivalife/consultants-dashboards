# Purpose

The Enterprise Human Evaluation Framework establishes the governance, operating model and execution standards for incorporating structured human judgement into the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

While AI Judges provide scalable automated evaluation, human evaluators provide contextual expertise, ethical judgement, business validation and final governance authority for critical enterprise decisions.

Human evaluation SHALL complement—not replace—automated evaluation.

---

# Objectives

The Human Evaluation Framework SHALL:

- Standardise human evaluation.
- Reduce evaluator subjectivity.
- Ensure consistent assessment.
- Validate business outcomes.
- Support governance decisions.
- Improve AI quality.
- Provide expert oversight.
- Support certification.
- Enable continuous learning.
- Maintain enterprise trust.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Prompt Reviews
- Agent Reviews
- Workflow Reviews
- Business Process Validation
- AI-generated Documents
- Knowledge Quality
- Customer Interactions
- Executive Reports
- High-Risk Decisions
- Enterprise AI Products

# Human Evaluation Principles

## Principle 1 — Human Accountability

Humans SHALL remain accountable for critical business decisions.

---

## Principle 2 — Structured Evaluation

Every evaluation SHALL use approved enterprise rubrics.

---

## Principle 3 — Evidence-Based Review

Human reviewers SHALL evaluate evidence rather than opinions.

---

## Principle 4 — Consistency

Equivalent scenarios SHALL produce consistent evaluation outcomes.

---

## Principle 5 — Explainability

Every human decision SHALL include documented reasoning.

---

## Principle 6 — Continuous Calibration

Evaluators SHALL be periodically calibrated against enterprise standards.

# Quality Gates

Human evaluation SHALL fail if:

- Required reviewers are unavailable.
- Evidence is incomplete.
- Rubrics are undefined.
- Consensus cannot be achieved.
- Governance approval is missing.
- Reviewer conflict of interest is detected.
- Audit records are incomplete.
