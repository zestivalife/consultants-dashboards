# Purpose

The Enterprise Quality Intelligence Platform establishes the architecture, governance and operational model for continuously measuring, analysing and improving AI quality across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Rather than viewing quality as an outcome of testing alone, the platform treats quality as a continuously evolving enterprise capability driven by telemetry, business outcomes, AI evaluation, governance compliance and operational intelligence.

Every AI capability SHALL participate in Enterprise Quality Intelligence.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Quality Intelligence Platform SHALL:

- Continuously measure AI quality.
- Correlate quality with business outcomes.
- Detect quality degradation.
- Recommend optimisation opportunities.
- Enable predictive quality analysis.
- Support enterprise governance.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Improve AI maturity.
- Support autonomous optimisation.
- Establish enterprise-wide quality standards.
