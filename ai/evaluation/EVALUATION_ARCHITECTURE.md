# Purpose

The Enterprise Evaluation Architecture establishes the enterprise-wide architecture for measuring, validating, benchmarking, governing and continuously improving every AI capability deployed within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Evaluation is not a post-execution activity—it is an always-on architectural capability that continuously measures quality, safety, business value, governance compliance and operational performance across prompts, agents, workflows, reasoning systems, models and business outcomes.

Every AI capability SHALL participate in the Enterprise Evaluation Architecture.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Evaluation Architecture SHALL:

- Establish a unified evaluation platform.
- Standardise evaluation methodologies.
- Enable continuous AI quality assurance.
- Measure business outcomes.
- Detect capability degradation.
- Support enterprise governance.
- Enable autonomous optimisation.
- Provide deterministic evaluation.
- Support auditability.
- Improve enterprise AI maturity.

# Architectural Principles

## Principle 1 — Evaluation by Design

Every AI capability SHALL include embedded evaluation mechanisms.

---

## Principle 2 — Continuous Measurement

Evaluation SHALL occur before, during and after execution.

---

## Principle 3 — Objective Assessment

Evaluation SHALL rely on measurable criteria rather than subjective judgement.

---

## Principle 4 — Business Alignment

Business outcomes SHALL have greater weighting than model-centric metrics.

---

## Principle 5 — Autonomous Improvement

Evaluation SHALL continuously drive optimisation recommendations.

---

## Principle 6 — Complete Traceability

Every evaluation SHALL be reproducible and auditable.
