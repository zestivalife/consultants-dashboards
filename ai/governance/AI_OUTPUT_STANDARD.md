# Purpose

The AI Output Standard defines the structure, format, completeness and quality expectations for all AI-generated deliverables within this repository.

Its purpose is to ensure every output is consistent, traceable, reviewable and suitable for enterprise engineering.

The Output Standard governs **how work is presented**, not how it is reasoned, decided or implemented.

---

# Mission

Produce professional, complete and auditable engineering deliverables that can be consumed by architects, engineers, product teams and stakeholders without additional restructuring.

---

# Vision

Every AI-generated artefact should look as though it was produced by an experienced engineering organisation following enterprise delivery standards.

Outputs should be:

- Consistent
- Complete
- Structured
- Traceable
- Reviewable
- Actionable

---

# Objectives

The Output Standard aims to:

- Standardise all engineering deliverables.
- Reduce ambiguity.
- Improve readability.
- Enable peer review.
- Maintain traceability.
- Preserve architectural decisions.
- Support governance audits.

---

# Output Principles

Every deliverable shall follow these principles.

## Complete

Provide sufficient information to complete the requested task.

---

## Structured

Organise information using predictable sections.

---

## Traceable

Reference related documents, decisions and dependencies.

---

## Actionable

Recommendations should be implementable.

---

## Concise

Avoid unnecessary repetition while preserving completeness.

---

## Explainable

Significant recommendations should include rationale.

---

# Standard Document Structure

Unless another template is explicitly required, engineering documents should use the following structure.

```
Title

Purpose

Scope

Background

Assumptions

Requirements

Design / Analysis

Dependencies

Risks

Recommendations

Implementation Notes

Validation

Related Documents

Revision History
```

Operational responses should generally include:

1. Executive Summary
2. Analysis
3. Recommendation
4. Risks
5. Dependencies
6. Next Steps

---

# Formatting Standards

Use:

- Clear headings
- Consistent terminology
- Ordered sections
- Markdown tables where appropriate
- Bullet lists for readability
- Code blocks only when necessary

Avoid:

- Unstructured text
- Duplicate content
- Ambiguous terminology
- Missing assumptions
- Undocumented decisions

---

# Traceability Requirements

Every major deliverable should identify:

- Related Governance Documents
- Related ADRs
- Related Specifications
- Related Workflows
- Dependencies
- Impacted Components

---

# Versioning
