# Purpose

The Knowledge Index provides the master catalogue of all enterprise knowledge assets managed within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

It serves as the primary navigation layer for people, AI agents and runtime services, enabling efficient discovery, governance and traceability across the enterprise knowledge ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

The Knowledge Index enables EAIOS to:

- Provide a single entry point to enterprise knowledge.
- Organise knowledge consistently.
- Improve discoverability.
- Support semantic navigation.
- Maintain traceability.
- Enable AI-assisted knowledge retrieval.
- Simplify governance and lifecycle management.

---

# Index Principles

The Knowledge Index shall be:

- Complete
- Accurate
- Governed
- Searchable
- Traceable
- Version controlled
- Continuously maintained
- Machine-readable
- Human-readable

# Lifecycle States

Knowledge assets may exist in the following states:

- Draft
- In Review
- Approved
- Published
- Active
- Archived
- Retired

Only Approved and Published assets shall be available for runtime consumption.
