# Purpose

The Best Practices repository defines the recommended methods, techniques and operational guidance for designing, developing, deploying, governing and operating the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Best practices capture proven organisational experience and enable consistent execution across people, teams and AI agents.

---

# Objectives

The Best Practices repository enables EAIOS to:

- Promote operational excellence.
- Standardise delivery approaches.
- Improve quality and consistency.
- Reduce implementation risk.
- Accelerate onboarding.
- Support AI-assisted execution.
- Encourage continuous improvement.

---

# Guiding Principles

Best practices shall be:

- Practical
- Proven
- Repeatable
- Measurable
- Technology-aware
- Governed
- Continuously reviewed
- Adaptable to changing business needs

They provide recommended guidance and do not replace mandatory policies or standards.

# Business Best Practices

Recommended practices include:

- Define measurable business outcomes.
- Align initiatives with enterprise capabilities.
- Maintain a single source of truth.
- Engage stakeholders early.
- Validate assumptions before implementation.
- Review business rules regularly.

---

# Product Best Practices

Recommended practices include:

- Maintain a prioritised product backlog.
- Validate requirements with users.
- Define measurable success criteria.
- Deliver incrementally.
- Capture user feedback continuously.
- Review roadmap alignment quarterly.

---

# Architecture Best Practices

Architects should:

- Design for change.
- Prefer modular solutions.
- Minimise coupling.
- Maximise cohesion.
- Reuse approved reference architectures.
- Document architectural decisions.
- Consider quality attributes explicitly.
- Review architecture before implementation.

---

# Engineering Best Practices

Engineering teams should:

- Write readable and maintainable code.
- Follow coding standards.
- Automate testing.
- Perform peer reviews.
- Keep pull requests focused.
- Manage technical debt proactively.
- Document non-obvious implementation decisions.
- Maintain high code quality.

---

# AI Best Practices

AI practitioners should:

- Ground responses using approved knowledge.
- Validate generated outputs.
- Maintain human oversight for critical decisions.
- Minimise hallucinations through retrieval.
- Protect sensitive information.
- Record evaluation results.
- Continuously improve prompts and agents.

---

# Security Best Practices

Security practices include:

- Apply least-privilege access.
- Protect secrets using approved vaults.
- Encrypt sensitive data.
- Perform security reviews.
- Monitor vulnerabilities.
- Rotate credentials regularly.
- Validate external dependencies.
- Maintain audit logs.

---

# DevOps Best Practices

DevOps teams should:

- Automate builds and deployments.
- Treat infrastructure as code.
- Monitor deployment health.
- Implement progressive delivery.
- Validate rollbacks regularly.
- Maintain environment parity.
- Measure deployment performance.

---

# Operations Best Practices

Operational teams should:

- Maintain runbooks.
- Monitor service health.
- Define service level objectives.
- Respond using incident playbooks.
- Conduct post-incident reviews.
- Measure operational performance.
- Review capacity trends.

---

# Data Best Practices

Data teams should:

- Maintain canonical data models.
- Validate data quality.
- Govern metadata consistently.
- Protect sensitive information.
- Maintain lineage.
- Archive obsolete data.
- Review retention policies regularly.

---

# Documentation Best Practices

Documentation should:

- Be concise and accurate.
- Use consistent terminology.
- Reference authoritative sources.
- Include ownership information.
- Maintain revision history.
- Be reviewed periodically.
- Remain synchronised with implementation.

---

# Governance Best Practices

Governance activities should:

- Assign accountable owners.
- Review changes before approval.
- Maintain audit trails.
- Track policy compliance.
- Review exceptions regularly.
- Retire obsolete knowledge.
- Measure governance effectiveness.

---

# Collaboration Best Practices

Teams should:

- Share knowledge openly.
- Reuse enterprise assets.
- Encourage constructive reviews.
- Document lessons learned.
- Resolve issues transparently.
- Promote cross-functional collaboration.
- Capture reusable organisational knowledge.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Best practices shall evolve through:

- Retrospectives
- Architecture reviews
- Engineering reviews
- Operational reviews
- AI evaluation
- Lessons learned
- Innovation initiatives

Continuous improvement shall be measurable and evidence-based.

---

# AI Consumption

AI systems shall use Best Practices to:

- Recommend implementation approaches.
- Improve generated artefacts.
- Validate proposed solutions.
- Detect deviations from recommended guidance.
- Support engineering and architectural reviews.

Best practices supplement mandatory standards but do not override them.

---

Best practices shall follow the lifecycle:

```
Proposal
```

---

# Quality Requirements

Best practices shall be:

- Proven
- Practical
- Actionable
- Current
- Reviewed
- Approved
- Traceable
- Measurable

Practices that become obsolete shall be revised or retired.

---

# Success Criteria

The Best Practices repository is successful when:

- Teams consistently adopt recommended practices.
- Delivery quality improves.
- AI-generated outputs align with enterprise expectations.
- Operational consistency increases.
- Rework and implementation defects decrease.
- Organisational knowledge continues to mature.
