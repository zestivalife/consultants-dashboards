# 1. Purpose

This governance document establishes the enterprise framework for identifying, assessing, managing, monitoring and continuously improving organisational risk across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Its purpose is to ensure that business, technical, operational, security, regulatory and AI-related risks are governed consistently through a structured, evidence-based and risk-informed decision-making framework.

Implementation requirements are defined within the Risk Management Standards that inherit from this governance document.

---

# 2. Governance Authority

The Enterprise Governance Board is the ultimate authority for Risk Management Governance.

The Risk Management Domain Owner is responsible for establishing, maintaining and continuously improving the enterprise risk management framework.

No Risk Management Standard may contradict this governance without formal approval from the Enterprise Governance Board.

# 6. Decision Rights

The Risk Management Domain Owner has authority to:

- Define enterprise risk policies.
- Maintain the enterprise risk framework.
- Require formal risk assessments.
- Escalate significant enterprise risks.
- Recommend risk treatment strategies.

The Enterprise Governance Board retains authority to:

- Accept enterprise-level risks.
- Approve governance changes.
- Approve strategic risk waivers.
- Resolve enterprise risk disputes.

---

# 7. Governance Model

Enterprise Risk Management shall follow the lifecycle:

1. Identify Risks
2. Analyse Risks
3. Evaluate Risks
4. Prioritise Risks
5. Define Treatments
6. Implement Controls
7. Monitor Risks
8. Report Risks
9. Continuously Improve

---

# 8. Governance Policies

The enterprise shall:

- Maintain an enterprise risk register.
- Perform risk assessments throughout the SDLC.
- Review significant risks before major releases.
- Continuously monitor enterprise risk exposure.
- Maintain evidence supporting risk decisions.
- Review control effectiveness periodically.
- Ensure AI systems undergo dedicated risk assessments.
- Integrate risk management into governance and operational decision-making.

Risk acceptance shall require formal approval from the appropriate authority.

---

# 9. Exception & Waiver Process

Risk-related exceptions shall:

- Be formally documented.
- Include business justification.
- Assess residual risk.
- Define compensating controls.
- Specify approving authority.
- Include review and expiry dates.

Permanent waivers are prohibited.

---

# 10. Escalation Path

Enterprise risks shall be escalated according to severity:

1. Team Lead
2. Domain Owner
3. Enterprise Governance Board
4. Executive Leadership

Critical enterprise risks shall be escalated immediately.

---

# 11. Roles & Responsibilities

### Risk Management Domain Owner

- Govern enterprise risk management.
- Maintain the enterprise risk framework.
- Review enterprise risks.
- Report organisational risk posture.

### Engineering Teams

- Identify implementation risks.
- Implement approved controls.
- Support risk assessments.

### Security Teams

- Assess security risks.
- Recommend mitigation strategies.
- Monitor security-related risk exposure.

### Product Owners

- Evaluate business risks.
- Prioritise risk treatment activities.
- Participate in release risk decisions.

### Enterprise Governance Board

- Provide strategic oversight.
- Approve enterprise risk acceptance.
- Review organisational risk exposure.

---

# 12. Governance Metrics & Reporting

Governance reporting shall include:

- Enterprise Risk Exposure
- High-Risk Items
- Risk Treatment Completion Rate
- Residual Risk Trend
- Open Risk Count
- Risk Assessment Coverage
- AI Risk Assessment Coverage
- Risk Acceptance Count
- Risk Review Completion Rate
- Enterprise Risk Maturity

Reports shall be reviewed quarterly.

This governance document establishes the mandatory enterprise governance framework for Risk Management across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).
