# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for creating, maintaining, governing and continuously updating the Enterprise Risk Register across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Its objective is to provide a single, authoritative repository for recording, tracking, reviewing and reporting enterprise risks throughout their lifecycle.

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Establish a single enterprise risk repository.
- Improve enterprise visibility of risks.
- Ensure complete risk traceability.
- Support governance decision-making.
- Enable effective risk monitoring.
- Strengthen regulatory compliance.
- Improve AI governance oversight.
- Facilitate audit readiness.
- Drive continuous risk management improvement.

# 4. Risk Register Principles

## 4.1 Single Source of Truth

The Enterprise Risk Register shall serve as the authoritative source for all enterprise risk records.

---

## 4.2 Lifecycle Traceability

Every risk shall remain traceable from identification through treatment, monitoring and closure.

---

## 4.3 Data Accuracy

Risk information shall be accurate, current, complete and periodically reviewed.

---

## 4.4 Ownership

Every recorded risk shall have a clearly assigned owner responsible for maintaining its status and associated information.

---

## 4.5 Continuous Maintenance

The Risk Register shall be continuously updated whenever significant changes occur.

---

## 4.6 AI Governance

Risks associated with AI systems shall be explicitly identified and managed within the Enterprise Risk Register.

---

# 5. Risk Register Lifecycle

Every enterprise risk shall follow the lifecycle:

1. Risk Identification
2. Risk Registration
3. Risk Assessment
4. Risk Analysis
5. Risk Treatment
6. Risk Monitoring
7. Risk Review
8. Risk Escalation (if required)
9. Risk Closure
10. Historical Retention

Each lifecycle stage shall be fully documented.

---

# 6. Risk Register Contents

Every registered risk shall include:

- Risk Identifier
- Risk Title
- Risk Description
- Risk Category
- Business Unit
- Asset or Service
- Risk Owner
- Date Identified
- Risk Source
- Business Impact
- Likelihood
- Inherent Risk Rating
- Existing Controls
- Residual Risk Rating
- Treatment Strategy
- Target Resolution Date
- Current Status
- Last Review Date
- Supporting Evidence

Mandatory fields shall not be left incomplete without documented justification.

---

# 7. Risk Classification

Risks shall be classified into appropriate categories, including:

- Strategic Risk
- Operational Risk
- Technical Risk
- Security Risk
- Privacy Risk
- Compliance Risk
- Financial Risk
- Vendor Risk
- AI Risk
- Reputational Risk

Organisations may define additional categories where justified.

---

# 8. AI Risk Register Requirements

For AI-related risks, the register shall additionally capture:

- AI System Identifier
- Model or Service Name
- AI Capability
- Human Oversight Level
- Responsible AI Classification
- Ethical Risk Assessment
- Bias Assessment Status
- AI Monitoring Status
- Model Version
- AI Governance Owner

AI risks shall be linked to applicable governance, monitoring and audit records.

---

# 9. Risk Register Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Controlled Access
- Version History
- Periodic Reviews
- Change Tracking
- Approval Workflow
- Audit Trail
- Evidence Repository
- Backup and Recovery

Risk Register integrity shall be protected throughout its lifecycle.

---

# 10. Risk Register Evidence

Evidence supporting the Enterprise Risk Register shall include:

- Risk Records
- Assessment Reports
- Analysis Reports
- Treatment Plans
- Monitoring Reports
- AI Governance Assessments
- Approval Records
- Review History

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

---

# 11. Metrics

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- Total Registered Risks
- High-Risk Items
- Critical Risks
- Open Risks
- Closed Risks
- Overdue Risk Reviews
- AI Risk Coverage
- Risk Register Completeness
- Risk Register Accuracy
- Average Risk Closure Time

# 12. Roles & Responsibilities

### Risk Owners

- Maintain assigned risk records.
- Update risk status.
- Support reviews.
- Maintain supporting evidence.

---

### Engineering Teams

- Register technical risks.
- Maintain implementation-related information.
- Support periodic reviews.

---

### Product Owners

- Register business risks.
- Validate business impact.
- Participate in governance reviews.

---

### Risk Management Team

- Maintain the Enterprise Risk Register.
- Govern register quality.
- Review completeness.
- Produce enterprise reporting.

---

### Risk Management Domain Owner

- Govern Enterprise Risk Register processes.
- Approve register standards.
- Review enterprise risk posture.
- Escalate significant risks.

---

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise initiatives governed by EAIOS.

All identified enterprise risks shall be recorded, maintained and reviewed within the Enterprise Risk Register.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.
