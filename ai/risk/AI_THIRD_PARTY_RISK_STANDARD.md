# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for identifying, assessing, managing, monitoring and mitigating risks associated with third-party organisations, services and technology providers.

Its objective is to ensure that external dependencies operate within the organisation's acceptable risk tolerance while protecting business continuity, security, privacy, regulatory compliance and AI governance requirements.

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Establish consistent third-party risk management.
- Protect enterprise assets and data.
- Strengthen supply chain resilience.
- Improve vendor governance.
- Reduce operational disruption.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Strengthen AI vendor governance.
- Improve business continuity.
- Enable continuous third-party oversight.

# 4. Third-Party Risk Principles

## 4.1 Risk-Based Selection

Third parties shall be evaluated according to business criticality, data sensitivity and operational impact before engagement.

---

## 4.2 Due Diligence

Appropriate due diligence shall be completed before contracting with any third party.

---

## 4.3 Continuous Oversight

Third-party risks shall be monitored throughout the duration of the relationship.

---

## 4.4 Shared Accountability

Business owners remain accountable for risks arising from outsourced services.

---

## 4.5 Evidence-Based Governance

Vendor risk decisions shall be supported by documented evidence and periodic assessments.

---

## 4.6 AI Governance

Third-party AI services shall comply with enterprise Responsible AI, security, privacy and governance requirements.

---

# 5. Third-Party Risk Lifecycle

Every third-party engagement shall follow the lifecycle:

1. Business Need Identification
2. Vendor Due Diligence
3. Risk Assessment
4. Security Assessment
5. Compliance Assessment
6. Contract Approval
7. Service Onboarding
8. Continuous Monitoring
9. Periodic Review
10. Contract Renewal or Offboarding

Each stage shall be documented and approved where applicable.

---

# 6. Third-Party Risk Assessment

Every significant third party shall be assessed for:

- Business Criticality
- Financial Stability
- Operational Capability
- Security Controls
- Privacy Controls
- Regulatory Compliance
- Business Continuity
- Disaster Recovery
- AI Governance
- Reputation Risk

Risk assessments shall be periodically reviewed according to vendor criticality.

---

# 7. Third-Party Governance Requirements

Enterprise third-party governance shall include:

- Due Diligence Reviews
- Risk Classification
- Security Assessments
- Privacy Assessments
- Contract Reviews
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Performance Monitoring
- Exit Planning
- Periodic Governance Reviews

High-risk vendors shall receive enhanced governance oversight.

---

# 8. AI Third-Party Risk Requirements

Where AI services are provided by external organisations, assessments shall additionally evaluate:

- Model Transparency
- Prompt Security
- Human Oversight
- AI Bias Controls
- Explainability
- AI Audit Logging
- Regulatory Compliance
- Model Version Management
- Data Residency
- Responsible AI Practices

AI vendors shall demonstrate compliance with enterprise AI governance requirements before production approval.

---

# 9. Third-Party Risk Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Vendor Risk Register
- Due Diligence Checklists
- Security Questionnaires
- Compliance Reviews
- Contract Management
- Periodic Assessments
- Continuous Monitoring
- Incident Reporting
- Exit Procedures
- Audit Trail

Controls shall be proportionate to the vendor's business criticality.

---

# 10. Third-Party Risk Evidence

Evidence supporting third-party governance shall include:

- Vendor Risk Assessments
- Due Diligence Reports
- Security Assessments
- Compliance Assessments
- Contract Approvals
- SLA Reviews
- Monitoring Reports
- AI Governance Assessments
- Vendor Audit Reports
- Offboarding Records

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

# 12. Roles & Responsibilities

### Business Owners

- Identify business requirements.
- Sponsor vendor engagements.
- Monitor vendor performance.
- Escalate significant risks.

---

### Procurement Team

- Coordinate vendor onboarding.
- Manage contractual documentation.
- Support governance reviews.

---

### Security Team

- Perform security assessments.
- Review vendor security posture.
- Monitor third-party security risks.

---

### Risk Management Team

- Govern third-party risk methodology.
- Maintain vendor risk reporting.
- Coordinate periodic reviews.
- Escalate significant vendor risks.

---

### Risk Management Domain Owner

- Govern enterprise third-party risk.
- Approve risk methodologies.
- Review high-risk vendors.
- Report enterprise third-party risk posture.

---

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all third-party engagements governed by EAIOS.

No third-party service shall be introduced into production without appropriate due diligence, documented risk assessment, required approvals and ongoing governance.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.

# 16. Standard Statement

This standard defines the mandatory enterprise requirements for managing third-party risks across all vendors, suppliers, cloud providers, AI providers and external service partners utilised by the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

All third-party relationships shall undergo structured due diligence, risk assessment, continuous monitoring and governance throughout their lifecycle to ensure secure, compliant and resilient business operations under the authority established by **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.
