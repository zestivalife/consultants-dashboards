# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for continuously monitoring organisational risks to ensure that risk exposure remains within approved tolerance levels and that emerging threats are identified, assessed and addressed promptly.

Its objective is to provide continuous visibility into enterprise risk posture through structured monitoring, reporting, escalation and improvement activities.

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Establish continuous enterprise risk monitoring.
- Detect emerging risks early.
- Monitor effectiveness of risk treatments.
- Improve organisational resilience.
- Strengthen governance visibility.
- Support informed decision-making.
- Enhance AI risk oversight.
- Improve regulatory compliance.
- Enable continuous risk improvement.

# 4. Risk Monitoring Principles

## 4.1 Continuous Monitoring

Enterprise risks shall be monitored throughout their lifecycle to ensure timely identification of changes in risk exposure.

---

## 4.2 Risk-Based Prioritisation

Monitoring activities shall prioritise risks based on business impact, likelihood, regulatory obligations and organisational risk appetite.

---

## 4.3 Evidence-Based Reporting

Risk status shall be supported by measurable evidence, operational data and documented reviews.

---

## 4.4 Proactive Escalation

Significant increases in risk exposure shall be escalated promptly to the appropriate governance authority.

---

## 4.5 Continuous Improvement

Monitoring activities shall identify opportunities to improve controls, governance and organisational resilience.

---

## 4.6 AI Governance

AI systems shall be continuously monitored for ethical, operational, regulatory and security risks throughout their operational lifecycle.

---

# 5. Risk Monitoring Lifecycle

Enterprise risk monitoring shall follow the lifecycle:

1. Define Monitoring Requirements
2. Collect Risk Data
3. Analyse Risk Trends
4. Validate Control Effectiveness
5. Update Risk Status
6. Escalate Significant Risks
7. Report Enterprise Risk Posture
8. Review Monitoring Effectiveness
9. Implement Improvements
10. Continue Monitoring

Each stage shall be documented and periodically reviewed.

---

# 6. Monitoring Activities

Risk monitoring shall include:

- Risk Indicator Monitoring
- Control Effectiveness Reviews
- Residual Risk Reviews
- Operational Monitoring
- Compliance Monitoring
- Security Monitoring
- AI Governance Monitoring
- Incident Trend Analysis
- Audit Finding Reviews
- Management Reporting

Monitoring frequency shall reflect organisational risk levels.

---

# 7. Risk Indicators

Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) may include:

- Number of High Risks
- Residual Risk Trend
- Open Treatment Actions
- Security Incidents
- Compliance Findings
- Operational Disruptions
- AI Governance Findings
- Vendor Risks
- Audit Findings
- Business Continuity Issues

Thresholds shall be defined for escalation.

---

# 8. AI Risk Monitoring Requirements

Where AI capabilities are implemented, monitoring shall additionally evaluate:

- Model Performance
- Prompt Behaviour
- Hallucination Trends
- Bias Indicators
- Human Oversight Compliance
- Explainability
- Security Exposure
- Privacy Compliance
- Regulatory Changes
- AI Operational Stability

AI monitoring shall integrate with enterprise governance and operational reporting.

---

# 9. Risk Monitoring Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
- Risk Dashboards
- Automated Alerts
- Scheduled Reviews
- Escalation Procedures
- Management Reporting
- Evidence Repository
- Audit Trail

Monitoring controls shall be reviewed regularly for effectiveness.

---

# 10. Risk Monitoring Evidence

Evidence supporting monitoring activities shall include:

- Risk Dashboards
- KRI Reports
- Risk Register Updates
- Monitoring Logs
- AI Monitoring Reports
- Security Monitoring Reports
- Compliance Reports
- Management Review Records
- Escalation Records

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

# 11. Metrics

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- High-Risk Exposure
- Residual Risk Trend
- KRI Threshold Breaches
- Risk Review Completion Rate
- Open Risk Treatment Actions
- AI Risk Monitoring Coverage
- Escalation Response Time
- Monitoring Accuracy
- Monitoring Coverage
- Risk Reduction Trend

- Update risk status.
- Escalate significant changes.
- Maintain supporting evidence.

---

- Report implementation issues.

---

### Security Teams

- Monitor security-related risks.
- Report emerging threats.
- Validate security controls.

---

### Risk Management Team

- Govern enterprise monitoring activities.
- Produce enterprise risk reports.
- Maintain monitoring dashboards.
- Coordinate governance reviews.

---

### Risk Management Domain Owner

- Govern enterprise risk monitoring.
- Review organisational risk posture.
- Escalate significant enterprise risks.
- Continuously improve monitoring effectiveness.

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise initiatives governed by EAIOS.

Enterprise risks shall be monitored continuously using approved monitoring practices, measurable indicators and documented governance processes.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.
