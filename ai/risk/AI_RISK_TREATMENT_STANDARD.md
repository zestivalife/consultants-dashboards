# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for selecting, planning, implementing, monitoring and validating risk treatment activities across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Its objective is to ensure that identified risks are addressed consistently using appropriate treatment strategies aligned with business objectives, organisational risk appetite and governance requirements.

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Standardise enterprise risk treatment.
- Reduce organisational risk exposure.
- Ensure proportional risk controls.
- Strengthen governance accountability.
- Improve operational resilience.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enhance AI risk management.
- Increase treatment effectiveness.
- Drive continuous improvement.

# 4. Risk Treatment Principles

## 4.1 Risk-Based Decision Making

Risk treatment shall be proportional to the level of assessed business risk.

---

## 4.2 Business Alignment

Treatment strategies shall support enterprise objectives while balancing cost, operational impact and residual risk.

---

## 4.3 Accountability

Each treatment plan shall have a clearly assigned owner responsible for implementation and ongoing effectiveness.

---

## 4.4 Evidence-Based Implementation

Risk treatments shall be supported by documented evidence demonstrating implementation and effectiveness.

---

## 4.5 Continuous Monitoring

Implemented treatments shall be monitored to verify continued effectiveness throughout the operational lifecycle.

---

## 4.6 AI Governance

Risk treatments involving AI systems shall include governance controls addressing ethical, operational, security and regulatory considerations.

---

# 5. Risk Treatment Lifecycle

Every enterprise risk treatment shall follow the lifecycle:

1. Review Risk Assessment
2. Select Treatment Strategy
3. Define Treatment Plan
4. Obtain Approval
5. Implement Controls
6. Verify Effectiveness
7. Update Risk Register
8. Monitor Residual Risk
9. Periodically Review
10. Close or Continue Monitoring

Each stage shall be documented and traceable.

---

# 6. Risk Treatment Strategies

Approved treatment strategies include:

- Risk Avoidance
- Risk Reduction
- Risk Transfer
- Risk Sharing
- Risk Acceptance

Selection of a strategy shall be based on business objectives, regulatory obligations and enterprise risk appetite.

---

# 7. Risk Treatment Planning

Every treatment plan shall define:

- Risk Identifier
- Treatment Strategy
- Business Justification
- Required Controls
- Responsible Owner
- Resources Required
- Target Completion Date
- Success Criteria
- Verification Method
- Residual Risk Expectations

Treatment plans shall be approved before implementation.

---

# 8. AI Risk Treatment Requirements

Where AI capabilities are implemented, treatment plans shall additionally evaluate:

- Human Oversight Controls
- Prompt Governance
- Model Validation
- Bias Mitigation
- Explainability Improvements
- AI Monitoring
- Security Enhancements
- Privacy Safeguards
- Operational Resilience

AI treatment activities shall maintain alignment with enterprise Responsible AI requirements.

---

# 9. Risk Treatment Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Treatment Plans
- Approval Workflow
- Control Implementation
- Residual Risk Evaluation
- Periodic Review
- Effectiveness Validation
- Risk Register Updates
- Audit Trail

Treatment controls shall be reviewed periodically for effectiveness.

---

# 10. Risk Treatment Evidence

Evidence supporting treatment activities shall include:

- Approved Treatment Plans
- Control Implementation Records
- Validation Reports
- Residual Risk Assessments
- AI Governance Assessments
- Review Records
- Approval Records
- Updated Risk Registers

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

---

# 11. Metrics

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- Treatment Completion Rate
- Overdue Treatment Actions
- Residual Risk Trend
- High-Risk Open Items
- Control Effectiveness
- AI Risk Treatment Coverage
- Treatment Verification Rate
- Risk Acceptance Rate
- Average Treatment Duration
- Treatment Success Rate

---

### Risk Management Team

- Govern treatment methodology.
- Review treatment quality.
- Maintain enterprise reporting.

---

### Risk Management Domain Owner

- Approve enterprise treatment methodology.
- Govern treatment activities.
- Escalate significant residual risks.
- Monitor enterprise treatment effectiveness.

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise initiatives governed by EAIOS.

Risk treatments shall be approved, implemented, validated and continuously monitored before associated risks may be considered controlled or accepted.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.
