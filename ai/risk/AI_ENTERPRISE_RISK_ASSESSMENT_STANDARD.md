# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for identifying, assessing, documenting and reviewing risks throughout the lifecycle of all business initiatives, software systems and AI-enabled services.

Its objective is to ensure that risks are evaluated consistently using a structured, evidence-based methodology that supports informed decision-making, governance and organisational resilience.

Governance authority, approval responsibilities, waiver processes and accountability are defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Standardise enterprise risk assessments.
- Identify risks early.
- Improve decision-making.
- Support governance activities.
- Reduce operational uncertainty.
- Improve project success.
- Strengthen AI governance.
- Maintain enterprise resilience.
- Enable continuous risk visibility.

# 4. Risk Assessment Principles

## 4.1 Risk Assessment by Design

Risk assessments shall begin during planning and continue throughout the lifecycle of every initiative.

---

## 4.2 Evidence-Based Assessment

Risk ratings shall be based on objective evidence rather than assumptions or opinions.

---

## 4.3 Business Context

Risk assessments shall consider business objectives, customer impact, regulatory obligations and operational dependencies.

---

## 4.4 Continuous Assessment

Risk assessments shall be updated whenever significant organisational, technical or operational changes occur.

---

## 4.5 Traceability

Every identified risk shall remain traceable from identification through treatment and closure.

---

## 4.6 AI Risk Consideration

AI-enabled capabilities shall undergo additional risk assessment covering ethical, operational, regulatory and governance considerations.

---

# 5. Risk Assessment Lifecycle

Every enterprise risk assessment shall follow the lifecycle:

1. Define Context
2. Identify Assets
3. Identify Threats
4. Identify Vulnerabilities
5. Assess Business Impact
6. Assess Likelihood
7. Determine Risk Rating
8. Recommend Treatments
9. Obtain Approval
10. Monitor Risk

Each stage shall be documented and retained.

---

# 6. Risk Identification

Risk identification shall evaluate:

- Business Risks
- Operational Risks
- Technical Risks
- Security Risks
- Privacy Risks
- Regulatory Risks
- Financial Risks
- Vendor Risks
- AI Risks
- Reputational Risks

Risk identification shall involve relevant business and technical stakeholders.

---

# 7. Risk Assessment Methodology

Each risk assessment shall document:

- Risk Identifier
- Risk Description
- Risk Category
- Business Impact
- Likelihood
- Inherent Risk Rating
- Existing Controls
- Residual Risk
- Recommended Treatment
- Risk Owner

Assessment methodology shall remain consistent across the enterprise.

---

# 8. AI Risk Assessment Requirements

Where AI capabilities are implemented, assessments shall additionally evaluate:

- Human Oversight
- Prompt Security
- Model Reliability
- Bias Risks
- Explainability
- Privacy Risks
- Regulatory Compliance
- AI Decision Traceability
- AI Operational Resilience

AI-related risks shall be documented within the enterprise risk register.

---

# 9. Risk Assessment Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Standard Risk Assessment Template
- Enterprise Risk Register
- Risk Classification
- Risk Rating Matrix
- Management Review
- Periodic Reassessment
- Approval Workflow
- Assessment Audit Trail

Risk assessments shall be reviewed before significant releases and organisational changes.

---

# 10. Risk Assessment Evidence

Evidence supporting risk assessments shall include:

- Risk Assessment Reports
- Risk Registers
- Business Impact Assessments
- Threat Assessments
- AI Risk Assessments
- Supporting Documentation
- Approval Records
- Review History

Evidence shall be retained according to enterprise record retention requirements.

---

# 11. Metrics

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- Risk Assessment Coverage
- High-Risk Findings
- Risk Review Completion Rate
- Residual Risk Trend
- Average Assessment Duration
- AI Risk Assessment Coverage
- Outstanding High Risks
- Risk Treatment Initiation Rate
- Assessment Quality Score
- Risk Register Accuracy

# 12. Roles & Responsibilities

### Risk Owners

- Identify and assess risks.
- Maintain risk records.
- Recommend treatment actions.
- Participate in periodic reviews.

---

### Engineering Teams

- Identify technical risks.
- Support assessment activities.
- Implement risk controls.
- Maintain technical evidence.

---

### Product Owners

- Assess business impact.
- Prioritise risk treatment.
- Support governance reviews.

---

### Risk Management Team

- Govern assessment methodology.
- Review assessment quality.
- Maintain enterprise risk register.
- Report enterprise risk posture.

---

### Risk Management Domain Owner

- Approve enterprise assessment methodology.
- Govern enterprise risk assessments.
- Escalate significant risks.
- Review enterprise risk trends.

---

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise initiatives governed by EAIOS.

Risk assessments shall be completed before significant implementation, release or operational change and shall be maintained throughout the lifecycle.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.
