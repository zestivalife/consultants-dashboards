# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for analysing identified risks to determine their likelihood, impact, severity and overall significance.

Its objective is to provide a consistent and evidence-based approach for evaluating enterprise risks so that treatment decisions are prioritised according to business objectives, operational resilience and organisational risk appetite.

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Standardise enterprise risk analysis.
- Improve decision-making.
- Prioritise organisational risks.
- Strengthen governance consistency.
- Support effective risk treatment.
- Improve enterprise resilience.
- Enhance AI risk evaluation.
- Enable measurable risk reporting.
- Reduce uncertainty across business operations.

# 4. Risk Analysis Principles

## 4.1 Evidence-Based Analysis

Risk analysis shall rely on objective evidence, historical information, expert judgement and measurable data.

---

## 4.2 Consistency

The same analytical methodology shall be applied across comparable enterprise risks.

---

## 4.3 Business Context

Risk analysis shall consider business objectives, customer impact, financial implications, operational dependencies and regulatory obligations.

---

## 4.4 Repeatability

Risk analysis shall produce repeatable outcomes when performed using equivalent information and methodology.

---

## 4.5 Transparency

Risk ratings, assumptions and analytical decisions shall be documented and available for governance review.

---

## 4.6 AI Risk Consideration

AI-enabled systems shall undergo additional analysis covering ethical, operational, security and governance risks.

---

# 5. Risk Analysis Lifecycle

Every enterprise risk analysis shall follow the lifecycle:

1. Review Risk Context
2. Analyse Likelihood
3. Analyse Business Impact
4. Assess Existing Controls
5. Determine Inherent Risk
6. Determine Residual Risk
7. Prioritise Risks
8. Recommend Treatments
9. Document Results
10. Submit for Review

Each stage shall be documented and traceable.

---

# 6. Risk Analysis Methodology

Risk analysis shall evaluate:

- Business Impact
- Operational Impact
- Financial Impact
- Customer Impact
- Regulatory Impact
- Security Impact
- Reputation Impact
- Technical Impact
- AI Governance Impact
- Recovery Complexity

Analysis shall be supported by documented assumptions where necessary.

---

# 7. Risk Rating

Enterprise risks shall be evaluated using a standard risk matrix considering:

- Likelihood
- Consequence
- Existing Controls
- Residual Exposure
- Risk Priority

Risk ratings shall be classified using enterprise-approved categories such as:

- Low
- Moderate
- High
- Critical

Risk scoring methodology shall remain consistent across the organisation.

---

# 8. AI Risk Analysis Requirements

Where AI capabilities are implemented, analysis shall additionally evaluate:

- Model Reliability
- Prompt Behaviour
- Hallucination Risk
- Bias Risk
- Explainability
- Human Oversight
- Security Exposure
- Privacy Impact
- Regulatory Risk
- Operational Dependency

AI risks shall be analysed independently from traditional software risks where additional governance is required.

---

# 9. Risk Analysis Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Standard Risk Matrix
- Documented Risk Ratings
- Independent Review
- Assumption Validation
- Evidence Repository
- Risk Prioritisation
- Management Approval
- Audit Trail

Risk analysis results shall be reviewed before significant implementation decisions.

---

# 10. Risk Analysis Evidence

Evidence supporting risk analysis shall include:

- Risk Analysis Reports
- Risk Rating Matrix
- Business Impact Assessments
- Supporting Evidence
- AI Risk Analysis Reports
- Review Records
- Approval Records
- Risk Register Updates

Evidence shall be retained according to enterprise record retention requirements.
