# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for identifying, assessing, analysing, treating, monitoring and governing risks associated with Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems throughout their lifecycle.

Its objective is to ensure AI technologies operate safely, ethically, securely, transparently and in alignment with enterprise governance, regulatory obligations and organisational risk appetite.

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Establish enterprise AI risk management.
- Minimise AI operational risks.
- Strengthen Responsible AI governance.
- Protect business and customer interests.
- Improve AI reliability and resilience.
- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Reduce ethical and reputational risks.
- Increase transparency and accountability.
- Enable continuous AI risk improvement.

# 4. AI Risk Management Principles

## 4.1 Human Accountability

Humans remain accountable for all business outcomes generated or influenced by AI systems.

---

## 4.2 Risk-Based Governance

AI controls shall be proportional to the business, ethical, regulatory and operational risks presented by each AI capability.

---

## 4.3 Continuous Assessment

AI risks shall be continuously identified, reviewed and reassessed throughout the AI lifecycle.

---

## 4.4 Transparency

AI risk decisions, assumptions and governance activities shall be documented and auditable.

---

## 4.5 Responsible AI

AI systems shall operate in accordance with enterprise Responsible AI principles covering fairness, explainability, privacy and human oversight.

---

## 4.6 Continuous Improvement

AI risk management shall evolve through monitoring, incident analysis, regulatory changes and emerging industry practices.

---

# 5. AI Risk Management Lifecycle

Every AI implementation shall follow the lifecycle:

1. AI Opportunity Assessment
2. AI Risk Identification
3. AI Risk Assessment
4. AI Risk Analysis
5. AI Risk Treatment
6. Governance Approval
7. Deployment Readiness Review
8. Continuous Monitoring
9. Periodic Risk Review
10. Retirement and Knowledge Retention

Each stage shall maintain documented governance evidence.

---

# 6. AI Risk Categories

Enterprise AI risk assessments shall evaluate:

- Ethical Risks
- Business Risks
- Security Risks
- Privacy Risks
- Regulatory Risks
- Operational Risks
- Model Risks
- Prompt Risks
- Vendor Risks
- Reputational Risks
- Financial Risks
- Data Risks

Risk categories shall be reviewed periodically as AI technologies evolve.

# 7. AI Risk Assessment Requirements

Every AI implementation shall evaluate:

- Business Purpose
- Human Oversight
- Prompt Security
- Model Reliability
- Hallucination Risk
- Bias and Fairness
- Explainability
- Privacy Impact
- Security Exposure
- Regulatory Compliance
- Operational Dependency
- Business Continuity

Risk assessments shall be completed before production deployment.

- Regulatory Changes

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all AI capabilities governed by EAIOS.

AI systems shall undergo documented risk assessments, governance reviews, monitoring and periodic reassessment throughout their operational lifecycle.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.

This standard defines the mandatory enterprise requirements for AI Risk Management across all Artificial Intelligence systems governed by the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

All AI capabilities shall be subject to structured risk identification, assessment, governance, treatment and continuous monitoring to ensure safe, secure, ethical and resilient operation while maintaining compliance with enterprise governance, regulatory obligations and organisational risk appetite under the authority established by **AI_RISK_MANAGEMENT_GOVERNANCE.md**.
