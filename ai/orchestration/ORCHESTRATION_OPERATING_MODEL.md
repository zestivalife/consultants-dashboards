# Purpose

The Enterprise Orchestration Operating Model defines how orchestration capabilities are governed, operated, evolved and continuously improved across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Where the Reference Architecture defines the structural design and the Service Catalog defines platform services, the Operating Model defines **how people, processes, technology and governance collaborate to deliver orchestration as an enterprise capability**.

This document establishes the operating principles, organisational responsibilities, lifecycle governance and operational cadence required for sustainable enterprise AI orchestration.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Orchestration Operating Model SHALL:

- Standardise enterprise operations.
- Define organisational responsibilities.
- Enable predictable delivery.
- Improve operational excellence.
- Support AI governance.
- Ensure service reliability.
- Promote platform ownership.
- Accelerate enterprise adoption.
- Enable continuous optimisation.
- Maximise business value.

# Operating Principles

## Principle 1 — Business Driven

Operational priorities SHALL align with enterprise business objectives.

---

## Principle 2 — Platform First

Shared orchestration capabilities SHALL be preferred over product-specific implementations.

---

## Principle 3 — Governance by Default

Governance SHALL be embedded into every operational activity.

---

## Principle 4 — Automation First

Operational processes SHOULD be automated wherever feasible.

---

## Principle 5 — Measurable Operations

Every operational activity SHALL be measurable through KPIs.

---

## Principle 6 — Continuous Improvement

Operational excellence SHALL be achieved through continual optimisation.
