# Purpose

The Enterprise Orchestration Analytics Standard defines the architecture, governance and analytical framework for measuring, evaluating and continuously improving orchestration across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The Orchestration Analytics Platform transforms operational execution data into actionable intelligence by analysing workflows, agents, tools, context, memory, knowledge, policy enforcement and enterprise outcomes.

Analytics SHALL provide both operational insights and strategic intelligence to optimise enterprise AI performance.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Orchestration Analytics Platform SHALL:

- Measure orchestration effectiveness.
- Evaluate execution performance.
- Analyse workflow efficiency.
- Identify optimisation opportunities.
- Predict operational risks.
- Support strategic decision-making.
- Enable continuous improvement.
- Measure business outcomes.
- Improve AI governance.
- Support enterprise scalability.

---

This standard applies to:

- Intent Engine

- Workflow Engine

- Knowledge Gateway
- Policy Enforcement Engine
- Execution Observability
