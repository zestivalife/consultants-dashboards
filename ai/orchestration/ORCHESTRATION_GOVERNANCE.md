# Purpose

The Enterprise Orchestration Governance Standard defines the governance framework for planning, controlling, auditing and continuously improving orchestration activities across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Governance ensures every orchestration decision, workflow execution, agent interaction, tool invocation and enterprise automation complies with organisational policies, security standards, regulatory obligations and architectural principles.

Every orchestration activity SHALL operate within an approved governance framework.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Orchestration Governance Platform SHALL:

- Govern enterprise orchestration.
- Standardise orchestration practices.
- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Enforce architectural standards.
- Maintain operational accountability.
- Manage enterprise risks.
- Enable policy-driven execution.
- Ensure audit readiness.
- Support continuous governance.
- Promote organisational maturity.

---

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- Intent Engine
- Execution Planner
- Agent Coordinator
- Workflow Engine
- Tool Orchestrator
- Context Orchestrator
- Memory Gateway
- Knowledge Gateway
- Policy Enforcement Engine
- Execution Observability
- Enterprise Analytics
- Human-in-the-Loop Processes

Every orchestration capability SHALL comply with this governance standard.

---

# Governance Principles

## Principle 1 — Governance by Design

Governance SHALL be embedded within orchestration architecture rather than implemented afterwards.

---

## Principle 2 — Accountability

Every orchestration decision SHALL have an identifiable owner.

---

## Principle 3 — Policy Compliance

Every orchestration execution SHALL comply with approved enterprise policies.

---

## Principle 4 — Transparency

All orchestration decisions SHALL be fully traceable.

---

## Principle 5 — Continuous Governance

Governance SHALL operate throughout the orchestration lifecycle.

---

## Principle 6 — Measurable Effectiveness

Governance effectiveness SHALL be continuously measured and improved.
