# Purpose

This workflow defines the enterprise standard for governing the complete lifecycle of a product from ideation through retirement.

It establishes lifecycle stages, investment decisions, governance reviews, transition criteria and retirement planning to maximise customer value while ensuring efficient allocation of organisational resources.

Every product SHALL be governed using this lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

- Govern products from inception to retirement.
- Align investment with lifecycle maturity.
- Optimise product portfolio performance.
- Enable evidence-based investment decisions.
- Reduce lifecycle risks.
- Improve cross-functional coordination.
- Support AI-assisted lifecycle analysis.
- Maintain complete lifecycle traceability.

---

# Trigger Conditions

Execute this workflow when:

- A new product idea is proposed.
- A product changes lifecycle stage.
- Quarterly portfolio reviews occur.
- Annual planning is conducted.
- Market conditions change.
- Acquisition or merger impacts products.
- Product retirement is considered.

---

The workflow SHALL NOT begin until the following artefacts are available:

- Corporate Strategy

- Product Backlog
- Customer Research
- Market Analysis

- Customer Success Metrics
- Risk Register

# Product Lifecycle Principles

- Customer focused

- Operationally sustainable

# Workflow Stages

## Stage 1 — Ideation

Owner: Product Manager

Activities:

- Capture opportunities.
- Assess strategic fit.
- Record assumptions.
- Define problem statement.
- Register opportunity.

Output:

Product Opportunity.

---

## Stage 2 — Discovery

Owner: Product Strategy Office

Activities:

- Conduct market research.
- Interview customers.
- Validate demand.
- Identify competitors.
- Estimate opportunity size.

Output:

Discovery Report.

---

## Stage 3 — Business Validation

Owner: Executive Product Committee

Activities:

- Validate business case.
- Review investment.
- Assess risks.
- Confirm strategic alignment.
- Approve incubation.

Output:

Business Case Approval.

---

## Stage 4 — Incubation

Owner: Product Owner

Activities:

- Build MVP.
- Validate hypotheses.
- Measure adoption.
- Collect feedback.
- Refine roadmap.

Output:

Validated MVP.

---

## Stage 5 — Growth

Owner: Product Director

Activities:

- Expand capabilities.
- Scale customer adoption.
- Increase revenue.
- Improve customer experience.
- Expand integrations.

Output:

Growth Report.

---

## Stage 6 — Scale

Owner: Engineering Director

Activities:

- Improve scalability.
- Optimise infrastructure.
- Expand operations.
- Improve automation.
- Increase resilience.

Output:

Scale Assessment.

---

## Stage 7 — Maturity

Owner: Product Strategy Office

Activities:

- Optimise profitability.
- Reduce operating costs.
- Improve retention.
- Maintain innovation.
- Review investment strategy.

Output:

Maturity Assessment.

---

## Stage 8 — Optimisation

Owner: Product Excellence Office

Activities:

- Reduce technical debt.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Enhance AI capabilities.
- Improve reliability.
- Optimise costs.

Output:

Optimisation Plan.

---

## Stage 9 — Retirement Planning

Owner: Executive Steering Committee

Activities:

- Assess end-of-life.
- Plan migrations.
- Notify customers.
- Archive systems.
- Retain required data.

Output:

Retirement Plan.

---

## Stage 10 — Retirement

Owner: Product Operations

Activities:

- Decommission services.
- Execute migration.
- Complete data retention.
- Archive documentation.
- Close governance records.

Output:

Product Closure Report.

# Mandatory Product Lifecycle Record

Every product SHALL maintain:

- Product ID
- Product Name
- Vision
- Lifecycle Stage
- Strategic Theme
- Business Owner
- Technical Owner
- Product Owner
- Customer Segment
- Revenue Model
- Investment History
- Roadmap
- KPIs
- Customer Metrics
- Operational Metrics
- Risk Register
- Compliance Status
- Revision History

---

# Lifecycle Entry Criteria

Each stage SHALL define:

- Required approvals
- Financial criteria
- Customer validation
- Technical readiness
- Operational readiness
- Governance approvals

---

# Lifecycle Exit Criteria

Each stage SHALL define:

- Business outcomes achieved
- Financial targets met
- Customer KPIs achieved
- Operational readiness
- Executive approval

---

# Investment Governance

Investment SHALL be reviewed based on:

- Strategic alignment
- Revenue growth
- Customer adoption
- Customer retention
- Gross margin
- Engineering cost
- Operational cost
- Cost of Delay
- Opportunity Cost

---

# Retirement Standards

Every retirement SHALL define:

- Customer migration
- Replacement product
- Support timeline
- Data migration
- Data retention
- Legal compliance
- Security obligations
- Infrastructure decommissioning
- Financial closure

---

# AI Lifecycle Standards

AI MAY assist by:

- Forecasting adoption.
- Predicting churn.
- Recommending investments.
- Simulating roadmap scenarios.
- Identifying lifecycle risks.
- Predicting retirement timing.

Executive approval SHALL remain mandatory.

---

# Product Health KPIs

Track:

- ARR / Revenue
- Customer Growth
- Customer Churn
- NPS
- Feature Adoption
- Release Frequency
- Engineering Velocity
- Operational Availability
- Gross Margin
- Customer Lifetime Value
- Technical Debt Ratio

---

# Quality Gates

The workflow SHALL pause if:

- Business case is incomplete.
- Customer validation is insufficient.
- Financial viability is unclear.
- Executive approval is absent.
- Retirement risks remain unresolved.

---

# Deliverables

Mandatory artefacts:

- Product Lifecycle Record
- Discovery Report
- Business Case
- Investment Plan
- Growth Assessment
- Retirement Plan
- Product Health Dashboard

---

# Exit Criteria

The workflow completes when:

- Lifecycle transition is approved.
- Governance records are updated.
- Stakeholders are informed.
- Product status is published.

---

# Escalation

Escalate:

Strategic conflicts → Chief Product Officer

Financial concerns → Finance Director

Architecture risks → Enterprise Architect

Operational issues → Product Operations

Compliance issues → Compliance Officer

Retirement approval → Executive Steering Committee
