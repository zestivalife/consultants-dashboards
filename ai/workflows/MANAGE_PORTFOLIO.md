# Enterprise Portfolio Management Workflow

**Workflow ID:** AI-WF-031
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** APPROVED
**Owner:** Enterprise Portfolio Office
**Classification:** Executive Governance
**Parent:** None (Root Workflow)

---

# Purpose

This workflow defines the enterprise standard for governing the organisation's complete product, programme and technology portfolio.

The Enterprise Portfolio aligns corporate strategy, financial investment, engineering capacity and business outcomes to maximise long-term organisational value.

Every strategic investment SHALL be governed through the Enterprise Portfolio.

---

# Objectives

- Align investments with corporate strategy.
- Maximise portfolio value.
- Optimise capital allocation.
- Balance innovation and sustainability.
- Govern enterprise risks.
- Optimise engineering capacity.
- Enable AI-assisted portfolio management.
- Maintain executive visibility.

---

# Trigger Conditions

Execute this workflow when:

- Annual strategic planning begins.
- Quarterly business reviews occur.
- Budget planning starts.
- New strategic initiatives are proposed.
- Mergers or acquisitions occur.
- Major market shifts occur.
- Executive portfolio reviews are scheduled.

---

# Required Inputs

The workflow SHALL NOT begin until the following artefacts are available:

- Corporate Strategy
- Enterprise OKRs
- Financial Plans
- Product Portfolios
- Product Roadmaps
- Market Intelligence
- Customer Insights
- Risk Registers
- Engineering Capacity Forecasts
- Regulatory Requirements

---

# Portfolio Principles

Every portfolio decision SHALL be:

- Strategy aligned
- Evidence based
- Customer focused
- Financially accountable
- Risk balanced
- Data driven
- AI assisted
- Continuously reviewed

# Workflow Stages

## Stage 1 — Strategic Alignment

Owner: CEO

Activities:

- Review corporate strategy.
- Define enterprise objectives.
- Confirm strategic themes.
- Establish investment priorities.
- Approve planning assumptions.

Output:

Enterprise Strategy Alignment.

---

## Stage 2 — Portfolio Assessment

Owner: Chief Product Officer

Activities:

- Review product performance.
- Assess programme health.
- Evaluate customer outcomes.
- Assess market positioning.
- Identify investment opportunities.

Output:

Portfolio Assessment Report.

---

## Stage 3 — Investment Planning

Owner: Chief Financial Officer

Activities:

- Allocate budgets.
- Define investment envelopes.
- Balance innovation and maintenance.
- Forecast ROI.
- Review financial constraints.

Output:

Investment Plan.

---

## Stage 4 — Capacity Planning

Owner: Chief Technology Officer

Activities:

- Forecast engineering capacity.
- Assess AI agent capacity.
- Review hiring plans.
- Assess vendor capacity.
- Balance delivery commitments.

Output:

Enterprise Capacity Plan.

---

## Stage 5 — Risk & Dependency Review

Owner: Enterprise Architect

Activities:

- Assess cross-product dependencies.
- Review strategic risks.
- Assess technology risks.
- Evaluate regulatory impacts.
- Define mitigation plans.

Output:

Enterprise Risk Assessment.

---

## Stage 6 — Executive Portfolio Review

Owner: Executive Portfolio Board

Activities:

- Review investments.
- Evaluate programme health.
- Assess benefits realisation.
- Resolve conflicts.
- Prioritise initiatives.

Output:

Portfolio Review Decisions.

---

## Stage 7 — Funding Approval

Owner: Executive Steering Committee

Activities:

- Approve funding.
- Confirm investment allocations.
- Approve strategic initiatives.
- Confirm delivery horizons.
- Publish executive decisions.

Output:

Approved Portfolio.

---

## Stage 8 — Continuous Governance

Owner: Enterprise Portfolio Office

Activities:

- Monitor portfolio health.
- Review investment performance.
- Measure strategic outcomes.
- Update executive dashboards.
- Publish governance reports.

Output:

Portfolio Governance Dashboard.

# Portfolio Segmentation

The Enterprise Portfolio SHALL classify investments into:

- Products
- Platforms
- Programmes
- Strategic Initiatives
- Innovation
- AI & Automation
- Technical Debt
- Security
- Compliance
- Infrastructure
- Research & Development
- Operational Excellence

---

# Mandatory Portfolio Record

Every portfolio SHALL include:

- Portfolio ID
- Portfolio Name
- Strategic Themes
- Corporate Objectives
- Investment Categories
- Products
- Platforms
- Programmes
- Strategic Initiatives
- Financial Allocation
- Capacity Allocation
- Value Streams
- Dependencies
- Enterprise Risks
- KPIs
- Executive Sponsors
- Governance Calendar
- Revision History

---

# Investment Governance

Investment SHALL be evaluated using:

- Strategic Alignment
- Expected ROI
- Net Present Value (NPV)
- Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
- Customer Value
- Cost of Delay
- WSJF
- Risk Exposure
- Time to Value
- Benefits Realisation

---

# Portfolio Capacity Standards

Capacity SHALL include:

- Engineering Teams
- AI Agents
- Product Teams
- UX Teams
- QA Teams
- DevOps Teams
- Vendor Capacity
- Operational Support

---

# Enterprise AI Governance

AI MAY assist by:

- Forecasting investment returns.
- Simulating portfolio scenarios.
- Optimising funding allocations.
- Predicting delivery risk.
- Detecting portfolio bottlenecks.
- Recommending strategic reprioritisation.

Executive approval SHALL remain mandatory.

---

# Portfolio KPIs

Track:

- Portfolio ROI
- Strategic Objective Achievement
- Benefits Realisation Rate
- Portfolio Throughput
- Budget Variance
- Capacity Utilisation
- Delivery Predictability
- Innovation Investment Ratio
- Technical Debt Investment Ratio
- Customer Value Index

---

# Executive Governance

Executive reviews SHALL occur:

- Monthly (operational)
- Quarterly (strategic)
- Annually (investment)

Emergency reviews MAY occur at any time.

---

# Traceability Standards

Every portfolio decision SHALL reference:

- Corporate Strategy
- Enterprise OKRs
- Strategic Themes
- Product Lifecycles
- Product Roadmaps
- Product Backlogs
- Epics
- Release Plans
- Financial Plans
- Risk Registers

---

# Quality Gates

The workflow SHALL pause if:

- Strategic alignment is unclear.
- Financial approval is missing.
- Capacity is insufficient.
- Enterprise risks are unacceptable.
- Executive approval is absent.

---

# Deliverables

Mandatory artefacts:

- Enterprise Portfolio
- Investment Plan
- Capacity Plan
- Executive Dashboard
- Portfolio Risk Register
- Governance Report
- Funding Decisions

---

# Exit Criteria

The workflow completes when:

- Portfolio is approved.
- Investments are allocated.
- Strategic initiatives are funded.
- Executive dashboards are published.
- Governance cycle begins.

---

# Escalation

Escalate:

Corporate strategy conflicts → CEO

Financial constraints → CFO

Technology risks → CTO

Architecture conflicts → Chief Enterprise Architect

Product conflicts → CPO

Governance disputes → Executive Portfolio Board
