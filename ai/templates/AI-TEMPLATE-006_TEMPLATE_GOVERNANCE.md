# Purpose

The Enterprise Template Governance Standard establishes the governance framework, organisational responsibilities, approval processes, compliance requirements and policy enforcement mechanisms for every template used within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The governance framework ensures that templates remain trusted, compliant, standardised and continuously aligned with enterprise architecture and business objectives.

Every enterprise template SHALL be governed under this standard.

---

# Objectives

The Template Governance Platform SHALL:

- Standardise governance.
- Ensure policy compliance.
- Protect enterprise quality.
- Improve accountability.
- Enable controlled evolution.
- Support AI governance.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Improve traceability.
- Maintain regulatory compliance.
- Enable autonomous governance.

# Governance Principles

## Principle 1 — Governance by Default

Every template SHALL be governed throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Principle 2 — Policy First

Templates SHALL comply with enterprise policies before publication.

---

## Principle 3 — Accountability

Every template SHALL have an accountable owner.

---

## Principle 4 — Complete Traceability

Every governance action SHALL be fully auditable.

---

## Principle 5 — Continuous Governance

Governance SHALL operate continuously rather than only during approvals.
