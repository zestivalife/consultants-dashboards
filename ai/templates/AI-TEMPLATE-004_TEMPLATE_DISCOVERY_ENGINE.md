# Purpose

The Enterprise Template Discovery Engine defines the architecture, services and algorithms responsible for discovering, recommending and retrieving enterprise templates across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The Discovery Engine enables humans, AI agents and enterprise platforms to rapidly locate the most appropriate templates using semantic understanding, metadata intelligence and contextual reasoning.

Every enterprise template SHALL be discoverable through this engine.

---

# Objectives

The Template Discovery Engine SHALL:

- Enable enterprise-wide template discovery.
- Support semantic search.
- Improve template reuse.
- Reduce duplication.
- Enable AI-assisted recommendations.
- Improve developer productivity.
- Support governance-aware search.
- Deliver contextual search results.
- Improve discovery accuracy.
- Support autonomous AI agents.
