# Purpose

The Enterprise Template Lifecycle defines the complete lifecycle management model for every template within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

It establishes how templates are created, reviewed, approved, published, maintained, versioned, deprecated and archived while ensuring governance, traceability and continuous improvement.

Every enterprise template SHALL follow this lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

The Template Lifecycle SHALL:

- Standardise template evolution.
- Ensure governance.
- Support version management.
- Improve template quality.
- Enable enterprise reuse.
- Maintain traceability.
- Support AI-assisted evolution.
- Reduce obsolete templates.
- Improve compliance.
- Enable continuous optimisation.

## Principle 2 — Version Integrity

## Principle 3 — Complete Traceability

Every lifecycle event SHALL be auditable.

## Principle 5 — Continuous Improvement

Templates SHALL evolve based on enterprise usage and feedback.

# Lifecycle States

## Idea

Purpose:

- Capture template requirement
- Define business objective
- Register proposal

Outputs:

- Proposal
- Business Justification

---

## Draft

Activities:

- Initial authoring
- Metadata creation
- Dependency identification

Outputs:

- Draft Template
- Initial Metadata

---

## Author Review

Validate:

- Structure
- Completeness
- Readability
- Enterprise alignment

---

## Technical Review

Validate:

- Engineering standards
- Architecture alignment
- Automation compatibility
- AI readability

---

## Governance Review

Validate:

- Policy compliance
- Naming standards
- Metadata standards
- Security requirements
- Regulatory obligations

---

## Validation

Perform:

- Schema validation
- Reference validation
- Dependency validation
- Metadata validation
- Quality assessment

---

## Approval

Approvers may include:

- Chief Architect
- Domain Owner
- Governance Office
- Security Office
- Enterprise Documentation Board

---

## Published

Actions:

- Version release
- Registry update
- Search indexing
- Knowledge indexing
- Notification publication

---

## Active

The template becomes:

- Enterprise approved
- Searchable
- Reusable
- AI consumable
- Governed

---

## Revision

Updates include:

- Improvements
- Bug fixes
- New standards
- Regulatory updates
- Feature additions

---

## Superseded

Occurs when:

- New version published
- Better template available
- Enterprise consolidation

---

## Deprecated

Conditions:

- Obsolete
- Unsupported
- Replaced
- No longer recommended

---

## Archived

Archived templates SHALL:

- Remain immutable
- Stay searchable
- Preserve audit history
- Support historical reference
