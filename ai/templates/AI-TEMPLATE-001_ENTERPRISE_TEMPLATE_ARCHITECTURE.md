# Purpose

The Enterprise AI Template Architecture defines the standard architecture for reusable templates used throughout the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Templates provide standardised blueprints for documentation, prompts, workflows, APIs, governance artefacts, engineering deliverables, architecture decisions, AI interactions and operational assets.

Every enterprise artefact SHALL originate from an approved template to ensure consistency, quality, governance and automation.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Template Architecture SHALL:

- Standardise enterprise artefacts.
- Improve delivery consistency.
- Enable reusable knowledge.
- Reduce implementation effort.
- Support AI-assisted generation.
- Enforce governance standards.
- Improve documentation quality.
- Accelerate enterprise delivery.
- Enable automation.
- Maintain architectural consistency.
