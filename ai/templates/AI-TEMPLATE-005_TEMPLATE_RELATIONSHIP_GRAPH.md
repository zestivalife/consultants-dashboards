# Purpose

The Enterprise Template Relationship Graph defines the canonical model for representing, analysing and governing relationships between templates across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The Relationship Graph enables AI agents and enterprise platforms to understand how templates connect, depend upon, inherit from and influence one another, allowing intelligent discovery, automated composition and impact analysis.

Every enterprise template SHALL participate in the Enterprise Relationship Graph.

---

# Objectives

The Template Relationship Graph SHALL:

- Model template relationships.
- Enable dependency analysis.
- Improve template reuse.
- Support AI reasoning.
- Enable impact assessment.
- Improve governance.
- Reduce duplication.
- Support semantic navigation.
- Enable intelligent recommendations.
- Provide enterprise traceability.
