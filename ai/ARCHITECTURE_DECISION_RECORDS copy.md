# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the Architecture Decision Record (ADR) framework for the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS). It defines how significant architectural decisions are proposed, evaluated, approved, implemented, reviewed and maintained throughout the lifecycle of the platform.

Architecture Decision Records provide a permanent history of architectural reasoning, ensuring that every major decision remains transparent, traceable and explainable.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Architecture Decision Record framework shall:

- Capture significant architectural decisions.
- Preserve decision rationale.
- Improve architectural transparency.
- Support governance.
- Reduce repeated discussions.
- Improve maintainability.
- Enable future architects to understand historical decisions.
- Maintain enterprise knowledge.

---

# 3. Scope

Architecture Decision Records apply to:

- Enterprise Architecture
- Repository Architecture
- Engineering Architecture
- Security Architecture
- AI Architecture
- Agent Architecture
- Workflow Architecture
- Knowledge Architecture
- Runtime Architecture
- Technology Selection
- Integration Architecture

---

# 4. Decision Principles

Every architectural decision shall be:

- Business Driven
- Evidence Based
- Technically Justified
- Governed
- Traceable
- Reviewable
- Version Controlled
- Reversible where practical

---

# 5. Decision Lifecycle

Every Architecture Decision Record follows the lifecycle:

Proposed

Under Review

↓

Approved

↓

↓

↓

- Strategic Alignment

- Compliance

Every decision shall reference:

- Related ADRs
- Architectural Components
- Repository Documents
- Standards
- Policies
- Specifications

---

# 14. Decision Validation

Approved decisions shall be validated through:

- Architecture Review
- Technical Review
- Security Review
- Governance Review

---

# 15. Decision Review

Every Architecture Decision Record shall be reviewed when:

- Business requirements change.
- Technology changes.
- Security risks emerge.
- Compliance regulations change.
- Better architectural alternatives become available.

---

# 16. Superseded Decisions

When an ADR replaces another:

- The previous ADR shall remain immutable.
- The replacement ADR shall reference the superseded record.
- Historical traceability shall be preserved.
