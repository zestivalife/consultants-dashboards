# Purpose

The Enterprise AI Agent Governance Standard establishes the policies, operating principles, accountability framework and oversight mechanisms required to govern AI agents throughout their lifecycle within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The objective is to ensure every AI agent operates safely, ethically, securely and consistently while delivering measurable business value.

Governance SHALL apply to every enterprise AI agent regardless of deployment model or autonomy level.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise AI Agent Governance Standard SHALL:

- Establish enterprise accountability.
- Govern AI agent behaviour.
- Enforce enterprise policies.
- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Support ethical AI.
- Protect organisational assets.
- Enable controlled autonomy.
- Measure business outcomes.
- Provide complete auditability.
- Enable continuous improvement.

---

# Governance Principles

## Principle 1 — Business Accountability

Every AI agent SHALL have a designated Business Owner responsible for its objectives and outcomes.

---

## Principle 2 — Technical Accountability

Every AI agent SHALL have a Technical Owner responsible for architecture, operations and maintenance.

---

## Principle 3 — Policy First

Enterprise policies SHALL override autonomous behaviour whenever conflicts occur.

---

## Principle 4 — Human Oversight

Critical business operations SHALL remain subject to human approval.

---

## Principle 5 — Continuous Governance

Governance SHALL be enforced continuously during execution rather than only during deployment.

---

## Principle 6 — Measurable Governance

Governance effectiveness SHALL be continuously monitored and improved.

| Level | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| A0 | Human performs all actions |
