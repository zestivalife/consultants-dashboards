# Purpose

The Enterprise Organisational Memory Standard defines the architecture, governance and operational model for preserving institutional knowledge across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Organisational Memory represents the highest level of enterprise intelligence. It captures enduring knowledge that belongs to the organisation rather than to individual users, teams or AI agents.

It enables the enterprise to retain institutional wisdom, strategic direction, governance principles and reusable knowledge across organisational change while ensuring explainability, compliance and long-term continuity.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Organisational Memory Standard SHALL:

- Preserve institutional knowledge.
- Maintain strategic continuity.
- Capture enterprise policies.
- Govern organisational intelligence.
- Preserve architecture decisions.
- Support enterprise reasoning.
- Enable knowledge reuse.
- Strengthen organisational resilience.
- Improve decision quality.
- Ensure regulatory compliance.

# Organisational Memory Principles

## Principle 1 — Enterprise Ownership

Organisational Memory SHALL belong to the enterprise rather than any individual, team or department.

---

## Principle 2 — Institutional Continuity

Knowledge SHALL remain available across personnel, organisational and technology changes.

---

## Principle 3 — Single Source of Truth

Organisational Memory SHALL provide authoritative enterprise knowledge.

---

## Principle 4 — Explainability

Every organisational memory SHALL preserve provenance, ownership and approval history.

---

## Principle 5 — Governance First

All organisational knowledge SHALL follow approved governance workflows.

---

## Principle 6 — Continuous Evolution

Institutional knowledge SHALL evolve through governed review, approval and versioning.

# Quality Gates

Organisational Memory SHALL fail publication if:

- Ownership is undefined.
- Supporting evidence is incomplete.
- Governance approval is missing.
- Classification is absent.
- Versioning is inconsistent.
- Security validation fails.
- Review schedule is undefined.
