# Purpose

The Enterprise Semantic Memory Standard defines the architecture, governance and operational model for managing validated concepts, facts, business knowledge and organisational intelligence within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Semantic Memory stores structured knowledge that is independent of individual conversations or events. It represents the enduring understanding of the enterprise, enabling AI systems to reason consistently using validated facts, concepts and relationships.

Semantic Memory SHALL serve as the authoritative memory layer for enterprise reasoning.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Semantic Memory Standard SHALL:

- Capture validated organisational knowledge.
- Maintain enterprise concepts.
- Store verified facts.
- Model semantic relationships.
- Support ontology-driven reasoning.
- Enable concept evolution.
- Integrate with the Knowledge Graph.
- Strengthen Retrieval Intelligence.
- Prevent contradictory knowledge.
- Support enterprise-wide consistency.

---

# Scope

This standard applies to:

- Enterprise Concepts
- Business Rules
- Policies
- Standards
- Architecture Principles
- Product Knowledge
- Domain Models
- Technical Terminology
- Regulatory Knowledge
- AI Knowledge Models

All validated enterprise knowledge SHALL conform to this standard.

---

# Semantic Memory Principles

## Principle 1 — Truth over Observation

Semantic Memory SHALL contain validated knowledge rather than raw observations.

---

## Principle 2 — Source Authority

Every semantic fact SHALL reference authoritative evidence.

---

## Principle 3 — Concept Stability

Concepts SHALL evolve through governed versioning rather than uncontrolled modification.

---

## Principle 4 — Relationship-Centric Design

Knowledge SHALL be represented through interconnected concepts rather than isolated records.

---

## Principle 5 — Explainability

Every semantic assertion SHALL be traceable to its origin.

---

## Principle 6 — Continuous Refinement

Semantic knowledge SHALL improve as organisational learning evolves.

Supported relationships SHALL include:

- Is A
- Part Of
- Depends On
- References
- Implements

# Fact Validation

Before publication, semantic facts SHALL be validated using:

- Authoritative Documents
- Approved Policies
- Architecture Decisions
- Product Specifications
- Regulatory Sources
- Subject Matter Experts

Unverified facts SHALL NOT enter Semantic Memory.

- Relationship Expansion
- Policy Changes
- Product Evolution
- Organisational Learning

# Quality Gates

Semantic knowledge SHALL fail publication if:

- Concept definition is incomplete.
- Supporting evidence is missing.
- Ontology mapping fails.
- Relationship validation fails.
- Authority cannot be verified.
- Versioning is inconsistent.
- Security validation fails.
