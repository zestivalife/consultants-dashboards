# Purpose

The Enterprise Team Memory Standard defines the architecture, governance and operational model for managing shared team knowledge within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Team Memory enables human teams and AI agents to collaborate through a governed, persistent and contextual memory layer that captures collective knowledge, objectives, decisions, artefacts and operational practices.

Unlike User Memory or Agent Memory, Team Memory belongs to the team as a whole and represents the team's shared intelligence.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Team Memory Standard SHALL:

- Preserve collective knowledge.
- Improve collaboration.
- Eliminate knowledge silos.
- Maintain project continuity.
- Capture shared decisions.
- Support multi-agent collaboration.
- Promote knowledge reuse.
- Strengthen organisational learning.
- Improve onboarding.
- Maintain governance.

---

# Team Memory Principles

## Principle 1 — Collective Ownership

Team memories SHALL belong to the team rather than any individual member.

---

## Principle 2 — Shared Context

Team Memory SHALL provide a common understanding of goals, decisions and ongoing work.

---

## Principle 3 — Knowledge Reuse

Reusable knowledge SHALL be promoted across projects wherever appropriate.

---

## Principle 4 — Controlled Contribution

Only authorised contributors SHALL modify Team Memory.

---

## Principle 5 — Explainability

Every team memory SHALL preserve provenance, ownership and change history.

---

## Principle 6 — Continuous Evolution

Team Memory SHALL evolve through collaboration, review and governance.

# Governance

The Enterprise Team Memory Standard SHALL be governed by:

- AI Platform Architect
- Enterprise Architect
- Team Owner
- Product Owner
- Security Architect
- Knowledge Architect

---

# Quality Gates

Team Memory SHALL fail publication if:

- Ownership is undefined.
- Team association is missing.
- Classification is incomplete.
- Provenance cannot be verified.
- Security validation fails.
- Required approvals are absent.
