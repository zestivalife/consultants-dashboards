# PERSONAS

## Purpose
Defines all platform personas, responsibilities and permissions.

## Personas

### Super Admin
- Platform owner
- Manage organisations
- Global configuration
- Full access

### Corporate Admin
- Manage organisation
- Invite users
- Manage practitioners and consultants
- Reports

### Consultant
- Manage clients
- Conduct assessments
- Create wellness plans
- Schedule appointments

### Practitioner
- Deliver assigned clinical/wellness services
- Update client records
- Review plans

### Mentor
- Guide consultants
- Review outcomes
- Knowledge sharing

### Client
- Complete assessments
- View plans
- Book appointments
- Track progress
