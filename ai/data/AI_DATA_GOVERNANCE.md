# 1. Purpose

This governance document establishes the enterprise framework for governing the creation, collection, storage, processing, sharing, protection, quality, lifecycle management and retirement of enterprise data across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Its purpose is to ensure that enterprise data remains accurate, secure, trusted, compliant and fit for business and AI-driven decision-making throughout its lifecycle.

Implementation requirements are defined within the Data Management Standards that inherit from this governance document.

---

# 2. Governance Authority

The Enterprise Governance Board is the ultimate authority for Data Governance.

The Data Management Domain Owner is responsible for establishing, maintaining and continuously improving the enterprise data governance framework.

No Data Management Standard may contradict this governance without formal approval from the Enterprise Governance Board.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Data Governance shall operate according to the following principles:

- Data as a Strategic Enterprise Asset
- Data Ownership and Accountability
- Data Quality by Design
- Privacy by Design
- Security by Design
- Regulatory Compliance
- Responsible AI Data Usage
- Lifecycle Management
- Evidence-Based Governance
- Continuous Improvement

# 6. Decision Rights

The Data Management Domain Owner has authority to:

- Define enterprise data governance policies.
- Establish enterprise data standards.
- Approve enterprise data classifications.
- Govern enterprise data quality requirements.
- Escalate significant data governance risks.

The Enterprise Governance Board retains authority to:

- Approve governance changes.
- Accept enterprise-level data risks.
- Approve strategic governance waivers.
- Resolve governance disputes.

---

# 7. Governance Model

Enterprise Data Governance shall follow the lifecycle:

1. Data Creation
2. Data Classification
3. Data Storage
4. Data Usage
5. Data Sharing
6. Data Protection
7. Data Quality Monitoring
8. Data Archival
9. Data Disposal

---

# 8. Governance Policies

The enterprise shall:

- Maintain an enterprise data inventory.
- Classify all enterprise data.
- Assign ownership for every critical dataset.
- Protect sensitive information using approved controls.
- Monitor enterprise data quality.
- Govern AI datasets independently where required.
- Maintain complete auditability of critical data changes.
- Integrate data governance into enterprise architecture and operational processes.

---

# 9. Exception & Waiver Process

Data governance exceptions shall:

- Be formally documented.
- Include business justification.
- Assess associated risks.
- Define compensating controls.
- Specify approving authority.
- Include review and expiry dates.

Permanent waivers are prohibited.

---

# 10. Escalation Path

Data governance issues shall be escalated according to severity:

1. Team Lead
2. Data Management Domain Owner
3. Enterprise Governance Board
4. Executive Leadership

Critical data governance risks shall be escalated immediately.

---

- Approve governance changes.

# Governance Statement

This governance document establishes the mandatory enterprise governance framework for Data Management across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

All enterprise data shall be governed as a strategic organisational asset using consistent ownership, quality, security, lifecycle and compliance practices. Supporting Data Management Standards shall implement these governance requirements while remaining fully aligned with the authority, principles and policies defined herein.
