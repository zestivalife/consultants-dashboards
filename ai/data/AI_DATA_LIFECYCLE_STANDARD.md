# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the mandatory enterprise requirements for managing data throughout its lifecycle within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Its objective is to ensure that enterprise data is created, maintained, protected, retained, archived and securely disposed of in accordance with business, legal, regulatory and Artificial Intelligence (AI) governance requirements.

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Establish a consistent enterprise data lifecycle.
- Ensure appropriate handling of data at every lifecycle stage.
- Improve data quality and integrity.
- Support regulatory and contractual compliance.
- Reduce operational and security risks.
- Improve AI dataset governance.
- Enable effective information management.
- Optimise storage and retention.
- Support continuous lifecycle improvement.

# 4. Data Lifecycle Principles

## 4.1 Lifecycle Governance

Every enterprise information asset shall follow an approved lifecycle from creation through secure disposal.

---

## 4.2 Business Ownership

Every dataset shall have an assigned business owner responsible for lifecycle decisions.

---

## 4.3 Security by Default

Appropriate security controls shall be applied throughout the entire lifecycle based on data classification.

---

## 4.4 Compliance

Lifecycle activities shall comply with applicable legal, contractual and regulatory obligations.

---

## 4.5 Traceability

Lifecycle events shall be auditable and fully traceable.

---

## 4.6 AI Lifecycle Governance

AI datasets shall follow enhanced lifecycle governance including versioning, provenance, validation and retirement controls.

---

# 5. Data Lifecycle Stages

Enterprise data shall progress through the following lifecycle:

1. Planning
2. Creation
3. Classification
4. Validation
5. Storage
6. Usage
7. Sharing
8. Maintenance
9. Backup
10. Archival
11. Retention
12. Secure Disposal

Each stage shall be governed by documented procedures and appropriate controls.

---

# 6. Lifecycle Requirements

For each lifecycle stage the organisation shall define:

- Business Owner
- Data Steward
- Security Controls
- Quality Controls
- Retention Period
- Access Requirements
- Audit Requirements
- Disposal Method

Lifecycle activities shall be documented and periodically reviewed.

---

# 7. AI Data Lifecycle Requirements

Datasets supporting AI systems shall additionally include:

- Dataset Registration
- Data Provenance
- Version Management
- Training Dataset Approval
- Bias Assessment
- Data Drift Monitoring
- Model Dependency Mapping
- Retirement Approval
- Historical Version Retention
- AI Audit Trail

AI datasets shall remain reproducible throughout their operational lifecycle.

---

# 8. Lifecycle Controls

Mandatory lifecycle controls include:

- Classification Validation
- Access Control
- Encryption
- Backup Management
- Version Control
- Audit Logging
- Retention Enforcement
- Secure Archival
- Secure Disposal
- Periodic Lifecycle Reviews

Controls shall be periodically assessed for effectiveness.

---

# 9. Data Retention

Retention periods shall be determined according to:

- Legal Requirements
- Regulatory Requirements
- Business Requirements
- Contractual Obligations
- Operational Needs
- AI Governance Requirements

Retention schedules shall be formally approved and reviewed periodically.

---

# 10. Secure Disposal

Data reaching end-of-life shall be disposed of using approved methods appropriate to its classification.

Approved disposal methods include:

- Cryptographic Erasure
- Secure Deletion
- Physical Destruction
- Media Sanitisation
- Certified Third-party Disposal

Disposal shall be documented and auditable.

---

# 11. Lifecycle Evidence

Evidence supporting lifecycle governance shall include:

- Data Inventory
- Lifecycle Register
- Classification Records
- Retention Schedules
- Backup Reports
- Archive Logs
- Disposal Certificates
- AI Dataset Version History
- Audit Logs
- Governance Review Records

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

# 14. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise information assets governed by EAIOS.

Enterprise data shall be managed throughout its lifecycle using approved governance, security, quality and retention controls.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.

This standard defines the mandatory enterprise requirements for managing enterprise data throughout its lifecycle within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

All enterprise information assets shall be governed from planning through secure disposal using consistent lifecycle management practices, security controls, retention requirements and audit mechanisms to ensure data integrity, compliance and responsible AI operations under the authority established by **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.
