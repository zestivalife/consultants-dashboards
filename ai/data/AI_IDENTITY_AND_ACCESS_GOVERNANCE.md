# 1. Purpose

This governance document establishes the enterprise governance framework for Identity and Access Management (IAM) across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Its objective is to ensure that every human identity, machine identity and AI identity is authenticated, authorised, governed, monitored and audited using consistent enterprise controls that protect organisational assets while enabling secure business operations.

---

# 2. Governance Authority

The Identity & Access Management Domain Owner has overall governance authority for enterprise IAM.

Governance decisions shall align with:

- Enterprise Security Governance
- Enterprise Risk Management
- Enterprise Compliance Governance
- Enterprise Data Governance
- Enterprise Architecture Governance
- AI Governance Framework

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Identity governance shall follow these principles:

- Identity First
- Least Privilege
- Zero Trust
- Default Deny
- Strong Authentication
- Role-Based Access
- Continuous Verification
- Complete Auditability
- Separation of Duties
- Secure by Design

# 8. Governance Policies

The organisation shall govern:

- Identity creation
- Identity verification
- Identity lifecycle
- Authentication
- Authorisation
- Role management
- Access provisioning
- Access revocation
- Privileged access
- API identity
- AI identity
- Identity monitoring
- Identity auditing

Identity decisions shall follow approved governance workflows.

---

# 9. Exception & Waiver Process

Exceptions shall:

- Be formally documented.
- Include business justification.
- Include risk assessment.
- Be approved by the IAM Domain Owner.
- Have defined expiry dates.
- Be periodically reviewed.

Permanent exceptions are prohibited.

---

# 10. Escalation Path

Identity governance issues shall be escalated as follows:

1. System Owner
2. Identity Administrator
3. Security Team
4. IAM Domain Owner
5. Enterprise Architecture Board
6. Executive Governance Committee

Critical identity incidents shall follow the Enterprise Incident Management process.

---

# 11. Roles & Responsibilities

### IAM Domain Owner

- Govern enterprise IAM.
- Approve IAM standards.
- Monitor governance effectiveness.
- Report IAM maturity.

---

### Information Security Team

- Enforce IAM security controls.
- Monitor identity risks.
- Conduct security reviews.
- Support audits.

---

### Engineering Teams

- Implement IAM controls.
- Integrate enterprise identity services.
- Maintain authentication mechanisms.

---

### Business Owners

- Approve business access.
- Review user privileges.
- Support periodic access reviews.

---

### Internal Audit

- Assess governance compliance.
- Review IAM effectiveness.
- Report audit findings.

# 16. Governance Statement

This governance document establishes the mandatory enterprise governance framework for Identity and Access Management across the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

All identities, authentication mechanisms, access decisions and identity lifecycle activities shall be governed through approved enterprise policies, standards and oversight to ensure secure, auditable and compliant access to enterprise resources.
