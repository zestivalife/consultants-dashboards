# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for measuring, maintaining, monitoring and continuously improving the quality of enterprise data throughout its lifecycle.

Its objective is to ensure that data used for business operations, analytics, regulatory reporting and Artificial Intelligence (AI) is accurate, complete, consistent, timely and trustworthy.

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Standardise enterprise data quality management.
- Improve decision-making through trusted data.
- Reduce operational and regulatory risks.
- Strengthen AI data reliability.
- Improve customer experience.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enhance data governance maturity.
- Enable continuous quality monitoring.
- Drive enterprise-wide data quality improvement.

# 4. Data Quality Principles

## 4.1 Accuracy

Data shall correctly represent the real-world entity, event or business process it describes.

---

## 4.2 Completeness

Required data elements shall be populated and maintained according to defined business rules.

---

## 4.3 Consistency

Equivalent data shall remain consistent across enterprise systems and integrations.

---

## 4.4 Timeliness

Data shall be available when required and updated within defined service expectations.

---

## 4.5 Validity

Data shall comply with approved formats, validation rules and business constraints.

---

## 4.6 Uniqueness

Duplicate records shall be minimised through appropriate identification, validation and governance controls.

---

## 4.7 AI Data Reliability

Datasets supporting AI development, training and inference shall satisfy enhanced quality requirements to minimise bias, errors and unreliable outcomes.

---

# 5. Data Quality Lifecycle

Enterprise Data Quality Management shall follow the lifecycle:

1. Define Quality Requirements
2. Profile Data
3. Measure Data Quality
4. Identify Quality Issues
5. Prioritise Remediation
6. Implement Corrections
7. Validate Improvements
8. Monitor Quality Metrics
9. Review Quality Performance
10. Continuously Improve

Each stage shall be documented and periodically reviewed.

---

# 6. Data Quality Dimensions

Enterprise data shall be evaluated using approved quality dimensions including:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Timeliness
- Validity
- Uniqueness
- Integrity
- Availability

Additional dimensions may be introduced where justified by business requirements.

---

# 7. Data Quality Requirements

Every critical dataset shall define:

- Data Owner
- Business Rules
- Validation Rules
- Acceptable Quality Thresholds
- Monitoring Frequency
- Escalation Criteria
- Remediation Process
- Review Schedule

Quality thresholds shall align with business and regulatory requirements.

---

# 8. AI Data Quality Requirements

Datasets supporting AI systems shall additionally evaluate:

- Data Representativeness
- Bias Indicators
- Dataset Completeness
- Label Accuracy
- Feature Consistency
- Data Freshness
- Privacy Compliance
- Ethical Suitability
- Training Data Provenance
- AI Dataset Versioning

AI datasets shall be validated before production use.

---

# 9. Data Quality Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Data Validation Rules
- Quality Dashboards
- Automated Quality Checks
- Duplicate Detection
- Exception Reporting
- Periodic Reviews
- Quality Audits
- Corrective Action Tracking

Quality controls shall be reviewed regularly for effectiveness.

---

# 10. Data Quality Evidence

Evidence supporting data quality activities shall include:

- Data Quality Reports
- Validation Results
- Profiling Reports
- Quality Dashboards
- Exception Logs
- AI Dataset Validation Reports
- Corrective Action Records
- Management Review Records

Evidence shall be retained in accordance with enterprise record retention requirements.

---

# 11. Metrics

The following metrics shall be monitored:

- Data Quality Score
- Accuracy Rate
- Completeness Rate
- Duplicate Record Rate
- Validation Error Rate
- AI Dataset Quality Score
- Data Quality Issue Resolution Time
- Quality Review Completion Rate
- Critical Data Quality Incidents
- Data Integrity Compliance

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise information assets governed by EAIOS.

Critical enterprise data shall be measured, monitored and continuously improved using approved quality methodologies and governance processes.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.
