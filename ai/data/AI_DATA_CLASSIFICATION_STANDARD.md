# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the enterprise requirements for identifying, classifying, handling, storing, transmitting and protecting enterprise data according to its business value, sensitivity and regulatory obligations.

Its objective is to ensure that all enterprise information assets receive appropriate levels of protection throughout their lifecycle while supporting business operations, compliance and AI governance.

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Standardise enterprise data classification.
- Protect sensitive information.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Improve data governance consistency.
- Strengthen enterprise security.
- Enable appropriate access controls.
- Improve AI dataset governance.
- Reduce information handling risks.
- Support secure data lifecycle management.

# 4. Data Classification Principles

## 4.1 Classification by Business Value

Data shall be classified according to its business importance, confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements.

---

## 4.2 Consistent Classification

A consistent classification methodology shall be applied across the enterprise.

---

## 4.3 Owner Accountability

Every significant information asset shall have an assigned owner responsible for classification and periodic review.

---

## 4.4 Protection by Classification

Security controls shall be proportional to the assigned classification level.

---

## 4.5 Lifecycle Classification

Classification shall remain valid throughout the information lifecycle and shall be reviewed whenever business requirements change.

---

## 4.6 AI Data Governance

Datasets used for AI development, training or inference shall receive explicit classification based on sensitivity, privacy and business impact.

---

# 5. Data Classification Levels

The enterprise shall classify information using approved classification levels such as:

## Public

Information approved for unrestricted public disclosure.

Examples:

- Public websites
- Marketing material
- Published reports

---

## Internal

Information intended for internal business use.

Examples:

- Internal procedures
- Project documentation
- Operational reports

---

## Confidential

Information requiring controlled access due to business, legal or commercial sensitivity.

Examples:

- Customer information
- Financial records
- Employee records
- Product roadmaps

---

## Restricted

Highly sensitive information requiring the highest level of protection.

Examples:

- Authentication secrets
- Encryption keys
- Government-protected information
- Highly confidential AI datasets
- Strategic acquisition information

Classification names may be adapted to enterprise requirements provided governance consistency is maintained.

---

# 6. Classification Requirements

Every classified information asset shall include:

- Data Owner
- Classification Level
- Business Purpose
- Regulatory Requirements
- Retention Requirement
- Access Restrictions
- Handling Instructions
- Review Date

Classification shall occur when information is created or acquired.

---

# 7. Handling Requirements

Handling procedures shall be defined for:

- Storage
- Transmission
- Processing
- Backup
- Sharing
- Archiving
- Disposal

Handling controls shall align with the assigned classification level.

---

# 8. AI Data Classification Requirements

AI-related information shall additionally identify:

- AI Dataset Classification
- Training Data Source
- Personal Data Usage
- Model Sensitivity
- Prompt Data Classification
- AI Output Classification
- Human Review Requirements
- Regulatory Restrictions

AI-generated outputs shall inherit classification requirements where applicable.

---

# 9. Classification Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Classification Labels
- Access Control
- Encryption
- Audit Logging
- Periodic Reviews
- Data Loss Prevention
- Secure Sharing
- Disposal Procedures

Classification controls shall be periodically reviewed for effectiveness.

---

# 10. Classification Evidence

Evidence supporting classification activities shall include:

- Data Classification Register
- Asset Inventory
- Classification Reviews
- Data Owner Approvals
- AI Dataset Assessments
- Access Control Records
- Audit Logs
- Governance Reviews

Evidence shall be retained according to enterprise record retention requirements.

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise information assets governed by EAIOS.

All enterprise data shall be classified, protected, periodically reviewed and handled according to its assigned classification level.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.
