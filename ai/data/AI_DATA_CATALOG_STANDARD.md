# 1. Purpose

This standard establishes the mandatory enterprise requirements for creating, maintaining and governing the Enterprise Data Catalog within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

Its objective is to ensure that enterprise data assets are discoverable, understandable, traceable and reusable through a centrally governed data catalogue supporting business operations, analytics, regulatory compliance and Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Governance authority, approvals, waivers and accountability are defined in **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.

---

# 2. Objectives

This standard aims to:

- Establish a central enterprise data catalogue.
- Improve data discovery and accessibility.
- Standardise business terminology.
- Support metadata governance.
- Improve data lineage visibility.
- Strengthen AI dataset discoverability.
- Increase data reuse.
- Improve governance compliance.
- Enable continuous catalogue improvement.

# 4. Data Catalog Principles

## 4.1 Enterprise Visibility

All approved enterprise data assets shall be registered within the Enterprise Data Catalog.

---

## 4.2 Discoverability

Data assets shall be searchable using business, technical and operational metadata.

---

## 4.3 Business Context

Every catalogue entry shall include sufficient business information to enable correct interpretation and usage.

---

## 4.4 Traceability

Catalogue records shall support complete lineage from source to consumption.

---

## 4.5 Ownership

Every catalogue asset shall have an assigned owner responsible for its accuracy and maintenance.

---

## 4.6 AI Catalog Governance

AI datasets, models, features and prompts shall be catalogued with additional governance metadata supporting transparency, explainability and lifecycle management.

---

# 5. Catalogue Contents

The Enterprise Data Catalog shall include, where applicable:

- Business Glossary
- Data Dictionary
- Dataset Inventory
- Metadata Repository
- Data Lineage
- Classification Information
- Data Quality Indicators
- AI Dataset Registry
- AI Model Registry
- Feature Store Metadata
- Integration References
- Ownership Information

---

# 6. Catalogue Requirements

Every catalogue entry shall include:

- Unique Identifier
- Business Name
- Technical Name
- Business Description
- Data Owner
- Data Steward
- Classification
- Source System
- Target Systems
- Data Lineage
- Quality Score
- Version
- Status
- Last Review Date

Catalogue information shall remain synchronised with enterprise systems.

---

# 7. AI Data Catalog Requirements

Catalogue entries supporting AI capabilities shall additionally include:

- Dataset Identifier
- Model Identifier
- Feature Definitions
- Prompt References
- Dataset Version
- Training Data Reference
- Bias Assessment Status
- Explainability Documentation
- Privacy Classification
- AI Governance Approval

AI catalogue entries shall support full operational traceability.

---

# 8. Catalogue Controls

Mandatory controls include:

- Metadata Validation
- Business Glossary Governance
- Version Control
- Lineage Verification
- Ownership Validation
- Access Control
- Audit Logging
- Periodic Catalogue Reviews

Controls shall be periodically assessed for effectiveness.

---

# 9. Search and Discovery

The Enterprise Data Catalog shall provide:

- Business Search
- Technical Search
- Metadata Search
- Classification Filters
- Data Owner Search
- Lineage Navigation
- AI Dataset Discovery
- API Discovery
- Dependency Mapping

Search functionality shall support rapid discovery of enterprise information assets.

# 13. Compliance

Compliance with this standard is mandatory for all enterprise data catalogues governed by EAIOS.

Enterprise data assets shall be catalogued, governed and maintained using approved metadata, lineage and ownership practices.

Exceptions shall follow the governance process defined in **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.

This standard defines the mandatory enterprise requirements for the Enterprise Data Catalog within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

All enterprise information assets shall be registered, catalogued and governed using consistent metadata, ownership, lineage and discovery practices to enable trusted, discoverable and reusable enterprise data under the authority established by **AI_DATA_GOVERNANCE.md**.
