# Purpose

The Enterprise Prompt Evaluation Framework establishes the enterprise-wide methodology for objectively measuring the effectiveness, quality, safety, consistency and business value of prompts executed within the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The framework provides quantitative and qualitative evaluation mechanisms to continuously improve prompts, reduce operational risk and maximise business outcomes.

Every production prompt SHALL be evaluated using this framework before deployment and continuously throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Prompt Evaluation Framework SHALL:

- Standardise prompt evaluation.
- Measure prompt quality.
- Improve response consistency.
- Detect prompt degradation.
- Evaluate business outcomes.
- Support governance.
- Enable continuous optimisation.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Support autonomous improvement.
- Provide measurable quality indicators.

# Evaluation Principles

## Principle 1 — Objective Measurement

Prompt quality SHALL be measured using standardised metrics.

---

## Principle 2 — Business Alignment

Evaluation SHALL prioritise business outcomes over model outputs.

---

## Principle 3 — Continuous Evaluation

Evaluation SHALL occur throughout the prompt lifecycle.

---

## Principle 4 — Explainability

Every evaluation result SHALL be reproducible and explainable.

---

## Principle 5 — Automation First

Evaluation SHOULD be automated wherever possible.

---

## Principle 6 — Governance Driven

Evaluation SHALL enforce enterprise governance policies.

# Quality Gates

A prompt SHALL fail evaluation if:

- Accuracy is below the approved threshold.
- Security validation fails.
- Governance compliance fails.
- Business objectives are not achieved.
- Safety score is below the minimum threshold.
- Performance exceeds enterprise limits.
- Cost exceeds approved budgets.
