# Purpose

The Enterprise Prompt Output Contract defines the mandatory structure, validation rules, governance controls and quality expectations for every response generated by the Enterprise AI Operating System (EAIOS).

The Output Contract guarantees that AI responses are deterministic, machine-readable, business-compliant, explainable and suitable for downstream automation.

Every Enterprise Prompt SHALL define an explicit Output Contract.

---

# Objectives

The Enterprise Prompt Output Contract SHALL:

- Standardise AI responses.
- Ensure predictable output.
- Enable machine consumption.
- Support workflow automation.
- Improve interoperability.
- Reduce parsing failures.
- Enforce governance.
- Improve response quality.
- Support auditability.
- Enable deterministic execution.

---

This standard applies to:

# Output Principles

## Principle 1 — Structured by Default

Every response SHALL conform to a predefined schema.

---

## Principle 2 — Human and Machine Readable

Outputs SHALL be understandable by users and consumable by software systems.

---

## Principle 3 — Deterministic

Equivalent executions SHALL produce equivalent output structures.

---

## Principle 4 — Governed

Outputs SHALL comply with enterprise security, privacy and compliance policies.

---

## Principle 5 — Self-Describing

Every response SHALL include sufficient metadata for interpretation.

---

## Principle 6 — Validated

Outputs SHALL pass schema and quality validation before delivery.

- Required fields are missing.
- Schema validation fails.
- Policy compliance fails.
